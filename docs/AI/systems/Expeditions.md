# Settlement expeditions

A settlement whose culture names an `ExpeditionProfile` keeps one band of 3–10 of its own residents out in the world at all times. Phase 1 (2026-10): the Scout goal only, a muster with no ceremony, nights as a `Halt`; no combat, camp or talk on the road.

## Model
| Owner | Where | Owns |
|---|---|---|
| [`ExpeditionDirector`](Assets/Game/Scripts/agents/Expeditions/ExpeditionDirector.cs) | on the `NpcWorldSim` object in `persistentScene`; server (`Network.Decides`) | every settlement's rotation (`SettlementState`) and every band (`ExpeditionRecord`), by `settlementId`, so bands run while the home chunk is unloaded; creates and steers the band's group; saves both |
| [`SettlementExpeditions`](Assets/Game/Scripts/agents/Expeditions/SettlementExpeditions.cs) | on each `Settlement` that runs bands; server | only what needs bodies: muster, walk-out, hand-off, walk-in, applying `Away`, the dead. **Keeps no state** |
| `NpcWorldSim` | beside the director | **moves** the band as a runtime group, `Owner = "expedition"`, template `settlement-expedition` |
| [`ExpeditionMember`](Assets/Game/Scripts/agents/Expeditions/ExpeditionMember.cs) | NetworkBehaviour on every resident prefab | a stand-in's replicated identity; a resident's lent kit weapon while it is with its band at home |

- **Phases** (`ExpeditionPhase`, saved int, append only): `Announced` (raised, members spoken for) → `Departing` (muster + walk-out, residents still home) → `Out` (handed off: stand-ins or folded) → `Returning` (at the hand-off point, walking in) → `Home` / `Lost`. `Ceremony` exists for Phase 2 and is never entered.
- **Rotation:** a settlement raises its next band when none is underway, or the only one has turned for home (`ExpeditionRules.NextBandDue`), so never three; it departs at `departHour` the next day.
- **Identity:** a resident's own body never leaves home. From the hand-off it is `Away` (offstage, hidden) and a **stand-in** — a fresh spawn of its `sourcePrefab` — walks the road. Members are the record's, never re-rolled; the dead are never spawned again. `residentKey` = `"r:" + Resident.index`; band id = group id = `exp:<settlementId>:<rotation>`.
- **Generic:** nothing under `Scripts/agents/Expeditions/` names a culture or species (`ExpeditionGenericityTests`); goals, kits, roles and the muster use are data off `SettlementCulture.expeditions`.

### Tunables (`ExpeditionTuning`; game minutes and hours of the day unless marked)
| Field = default | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `travelDawn` 6 · `travelDusk` 19 | a band walks only between these hours; a Halt ends at the next dawn |
| `departHour` 7.5 · `musterMinutes` 30 | when the band musters; how long it stands at the spot |
| `musterInset` 5 m · `musterSpacing` 1.2 m | rule-placed spot's distance back from the paving end; gap between members in the row |
| `departRadius` 150 m · `maxHandoffDistance` 400 m | unobserved hand-off past this; hand-off here even watched. Both from the muster spot; `handoffPoint` = spot + forward × `departRadius` |
| `handoffObserveRadius` 400 m · `observeRadius` 150 m | "watched" for the walk-out, capped at `NpcWorldSim.DespawnRadius` (`ExpeditionDirector.HandOffObserveRadius`, so 360 m in `persistentScene`); for the folded walk-in's appearance (`ObserverCheck.AnyPlayerSees`) |
| `walkLimitMinutes` 240 | a walk-out is handed off, a walk-in is home, after this however far it got |
| `restDays` 2 · `minHomeShare` 0.8 | a member is picked again from `homeDay + restDays + 1`; share of residents that must stay home |
| `varietyPenalty` 0.5 · `decisionInterval` 0.5 **real s** | weight factor on the last goal; director and performer pass period |
| `searchRing` 150–300 m · `travelReach` 800–1600 m | Search waypoint radius; Travel distance (a `travelSiteKinds` site — Ruin, ScrapField, Landmark — in that band is preferred) |
| `arriveRadius` 15 m · `destinationSampleDistance` 25 m | arrival; NavMesh sample round any destination |
| `spawnedLegLength` 120 m · `leaderStopRadius` 5 m | longest leg a spawned band is sent at once (Flows 6); where its leader is told to stop, inside `arriveRadius` (Gotchas) |

Elsewhere: profile `warriorQuotaSmall` 6–8 (equal bed bands), `warriorQuotaLarge` 12 from `largeFromBeds` 40, half kept home (`WarriorsHomeMin`); `NomadExpeditions.roleQuotas` = Scout 1–2 small, 3 large (Scout and Lookout archetypes); director `simulateStepMinutes` 10, `LegShortening` 0.5 (a const); template `travelSpeed` 3.5 m/s, `Column`. `Goal_Scout`: weight 3, Warrior 2–2 (`Tool_Spear_Bone` + `Tool_SignalHorn`) + Scout 1–2, `fillFromAnyAdult` (`Tool_Spear_Stone` + `Tool_Spyglass`); Travel, Search (240–480 min, 3–5 waypoints), Halt, twice, then ReturnHome.

## Flows
1. **Raise** (`Rotate`, each pass, not while held after a load): `TryRaise` needs the settlement's catalog entry with `hasMuster`; draws a goal (seeded `BandSeed(settlementId, rotation)`, last goal × `varietyPenalty`) and members (`TryPickMembers`: home, adult, rested, not spoken for, quota and `minHomeShare` kept, bonds preferred, a Warrior leads); an unfillable goal is passed over. Failure warns once per day.
   - **Who fills a slot:** residents with its role, every slot's `min` then every `max`; then a `fillFromAnyAdult` slot still short of its `min` takes any free adult for the rest (never a warrior the `WarriorsHomeMin` needs). Such a **stand-in for a role** keeps its archetype (it is still the potter) but draws the slot role's kit and has the slot's role in `MemberRecord.role`. Every member's `role` is its slot's role.
2. **Depart** at `departHour` on `departDay`: a performed settlement's band turns `Departing`; an unloaded one's `DepartWithoutBodies` (group at `handoffPoint`, `Out`, absence raised).
3. **Muster** (performer): members `ComeOnstage`, `CarryKit` (kit weapon lent, presence `Activity.Expedition`), `Scripted` override to `MusterRow` at `Society.TryMusterPose` (hold cue `bored` from `Muster.asset`). No usable spot or no living cast → `LeaveWithoutMuster`: hand-off at the road point, residents `GoAway` where they stand, a warning.
4. **Walk-out:** the leader says a `Farewell` line (the culture's `depart.*` rows); the row walks to `maxHandoffDistance + arriveRadius` along the road. `HandOffNow` = past `departRadius` unwatched (watched = a player with a line of sight within `HandOffObserveRadius`), or at `maxHandoffDistance`, or `walkLimitMinutes` elapsed.
5. **Hand-off**, one call: `BeginHandOff(id, poses by member)` creates the group at the centroid, sets the Travel target and, **if a player is within `NpcWorldSim.DespawnRadius`**, sets `SpawnPoses` and `SpawnNow`; the performer then `GoAway`s every member on the same server tick.
6. **Road** (`Travel` each pass): stand-in health into the record → `ExpeditionRules.Advance` (game-minute timers, arrivals; a clock jump settles every timed end in order) → `ChooseTarget` (Travel: seeded site or ring point, sampled, saved in `target`; Search: circles where it stands) → `Move`: folded, `GoalPosition`/`HasGoal` (travel hours only) that `TickVirtual` walks, the whole way in one go; spawned, `SteerSpawned` the leader **one leg at a time** (`LegOf`): `ExpeditionRules.LegPoint` from the group's live centroid, at most `spawnedLegLength` along the straight line to the destination, NavMesh-sampled (`destinationSampleDistance`; no ground there → the leg is halved, down to `arriveRadius`); the next leg once any member is within `arriveRadius` of the leg's end; the last leg is the destination itself. Stage arrival is still judged against the destination. A halt or a fold drops the leg; walking on starts a new one from where the band stands. The leader is told to stop at `leaderStopRadius`.
7. **Stand-ins:** `NpcGroupComposition.Resolve` takes `PlannedOverride` (living members' source prefabs). `MemberStamp` (before network spawn) → `ExpeditionMember.Stamp` (name, archetype, kit, `SetFormation(bandId, leader)` + enable) and the record's health; `HealthComponent.OnDeath` (not while `IsRestoring`) marks the member dead and re-plans. `ReadBack` on a fold copies health back. All dead → `Lost`.
8. **Return:** ReturnHome arrives → `Returning`; unloaded → `Home` at once. Performed → `Appear`: spawned, each resident `ComeHome`s at its stand-in's pose and the group is disbanded in the same call; folded, they appear in a row at the road point once nobody watches it. `WalkIn` to the muster spot → `CompleteHomecoming` (`Home`, members rest).
9. **Finished bands** with dead stay until a performer applies them (`DieAtHome`: `GoAway` + `RestoreHealth(0)`, a restored death: no loot, no fall) and `Retire`s the record. A dead member with no resident in the scene is warned about (band and key); the record is kept while the world's catalog roster (`CatalogRosterOf`) still lists the key, retired once it does not (`ExpeditionRules.MayRetire`).
10. **Absence:** `IsAway` = member of an `Out`/`Returning` band, or dead in an unretired finished one. The performer's start pass (its first `Update`) and every `AbsenceChanged` make residents agree (`GoAway` where they stand, or `ComeHome`); the society re-plans on the same event.

## Multiplayer
- Director, performer, rotation and `/exp` handlers: server only. Clients see ordinary replicated bodies.
- Stand-ins are `NpcSpawn` spawns of resident prefabs (must be in the network prefab list). `ExpeditionMember` replicates `residentKey`, `displayName` and four `short` indices (profile, goal, archetype, kit); every machine resolves archetype and kit from the `ExpeditionCatalog` and packs the same bag — the bag is never sent.
- `Away` is the presence's replicated `hidden` (+ `Activity.Sleep`); with the band it is `Activity.Expedition`, which every machine's hand rule turns into the kit weapon (`Resident.HeldItem`). The departure line is `ResidentSaid`.

## Persistence
- Key **`expeditions`** ([ExpeditionSaveable](Assets/Game/Scripts/Core/Persistence/Adapters/ExpeditionSaveable.cs)) beside `NpcWorldSaveable` on the `NpcWorldSim` entity: `settlements[]` + `bands[]`. The group is saved under `npcworld` with `owner = "expedition"`; `ClaimRuntimeOwner` (director `Awake`) keeps it on restore.
- Not saved: stand-ins (`NpcSpawn`, save-invisible), `Resident.away`, performances, lent weapons (`ILentSlots`).
- **Restore:** `Departing` → `Out` (a missing group is re-created at `handoffPoint`); `Returning` → `Home`; the rotation is held until `WorldSaveStore.OnSceneHydrated(Persistent)`; `AbsenceChanged` fires for every settlement. Seeds come from `(settlementId, rotation)` and `(seed, stageIndex)`, so a reload re-rolls nothing.

## Gotchas
- **`SaveManager.OnLoadApplied` is the wrong "load finished" signal.** It fires only when a binding player had a record, so a world saved before anyone had one never released the rotation hold: no band ever again. The hold waits for `WorldSaveStore.OnSceneHydrated(SceneKey.Persistent)`, which runs on every load.
- **`Resident.away` is not saved; the performer re-applies it.** Until its start pass runs, a member of an `Out` band stands at home after a load. The pass runs in the first `Update`, spawned or not: `ResidentPresence` writes what was published before its network spawn into its NetworkVariables in `OnNetworkSpawn`. Until 2026-10-04 it did not, so a `GoAway` before the spawn hid the body on the host only and the pass had to wait for every presence `IsSpawned`.
- **`AdoptGroups` re-creates a missing group for every band past the hand-off — except a performed `Returning` one.** `Appear` disbanded it on purpose; re-creating it put stand-ins beside the residents walking in.
- **A watcher only counts while the hand-off would spawn stand-ins for them.** `BeginHandOff` spawns them only with a player within `NpcWorldSim.DespawnRadius` (360 m in `persistentScene`), while `handoffObserveRadius` and `maxHandoffDistance` are 400 m: a watcher 360–400 m off used to hold the band to the 400 m swap, where it handed off folded and the residents vanished in view. The performer now asks `ExpeditionDirector.HandOffObserveRadius` = `min(handoffObserveRadius, DespawnRadius)` (`ExpeditionRules.EffectiveObserveRadius`): a farther watcher sees the band handed off at `departRadius`, the same fold in view the sim gives any group past its despawn radius. Left: the performer measures each member, `BeginHandOff` the band's centroid, so a watcher within a metre or two of 360 m can still fall between them. Unmeasured in play.
- **A spawned band sent a kilometre never moved (play test, 2026-10-04): it walks legs of `spawnedLegLength` now.** Measured: leader given a Travel target 1.1 km off kept `pathPending=True`, `hasPath=False`, velocity 0 for over a minute; the same group steered 60 m walked at once. Cause, by reading the code (not measured): `NavMeshAgentMotor.ApplyMoveIntent` calls `SetDestination` whenever `!agent.hasPath`, and `hasPath` is false while a path is still pending; `GoalTravelModule` emits its `MoveTo` every tick, so the async path query restarts every frame and one that needs more than a frame's `NavMesh.pathfindingIterationsPerFrame` never finishes. Short paths finish in one frame, which is why every errand works. The motor now skips re-requests while `pathPending` (AgentSystem.md Gotchas); the legs stay, since a world-wide path is still costly to compute in one piece.
- **The leader is told to stop at `leaderStopRadius` (5 m), not `arriveRadius`.** `GoalTravelModule` stops the agent at the goal's radius + its `stopDistanceMargin`, measured along the path (`remainingDistance`), so a leader steered with the 15 m arrive radius could stand 15.3 m off on flat ground — outside `HasArrived`'s 15 m — and the band would wait there for ever. Unmeasured in play.
- **Road behaviour keys on `MemberRecord.role`, never on the archetype's roles.** A stand-in for a role (`fillFromAnyAdult`) keeps its own archetype, so its `expeditionRoles` say nothing about what it does on the road; its kit row (`kitIndex`) is already the slot role's. Records saved before `role` existed load with `None`. The leader is still drawn from the members whose archetype is a Warrior.
- **Generate guarantees a quota only from copies whose role is CERTAIN.** A body with no role of its own counts only when every archetype `ResidentAssignment.Assign` could deal it has the role, and at least one has no standing duty (dealt from guards alone it gets none once the patrol is full); `Raxy_Warrior` (Guard, TowerGuard, Hunter, Butcher) does not count, so `NomadSettlement.asset` (only role-less bodies) still relies on Apply Role Quotas. `RaxySettlement.asset` (`Tools/SpaceGame/Residents/Author Raxy Settlement Config`, `RaxySettlementConfigMenu`) lists every Raxy resident prefab; its quota bodies roll at chance 1 and those needing no spot cover the 70-bed quotas alone (13 warriors for 12, 5 scouts for 3; plus 2 TowerGuard, 2 Lookout where a tower or gate exists). A seeded simulation of that roll (2000 seeds) gave 73–100 copies for 70 beds and 12–15 certain warriors, 3–7 scouts. Regenerating a settlement whose quota was already met by the shuffle moves the same people in; one that was short gets different people (old saves' records orphaned, Residents.md Persistence).
- **Role quotas recast residents; they do not change who may go.** The director keeps no role quota at runtime (only `WarriorsHomeMin` from the warrior quota). `Apply Role Quotas` runs the warrior quota first, then each role quota over the archetypes the earlier ones left, and never recasts a warrior or anybody twice in one run. The 0.5 warrior picks recast the settlement's second Scout (#56) to Guard, leaving one: after one scouting trip (rest 3 days) no Scout band could form — the reason for the Scout quota and the stand-in flag.
- **An unwatched band skips its walk-out.** A clock jump rebuilds the plans, and a resident's next pass (`ResidentRoutine.Settle`) teleports it to its goal when it is over 30 m off and no player is within 60 m of it or of the goal; during the walk-out that goal is the walk-out row 415 m out, so the next performer pass hands the band off there, unwatched. The `expedition-*` autotests' `HANDOFF_BY=the walk-out` at 415 m is this, not a watcher (read from the code; it matches the 2026-10-04 host and persist logs, where the hand-off came seconds after the jump). The old layout's muster spot was ~43 m from the arrival ship at the spawn point, inside that 60 m, so its band walked instead (inferred).
- **A group with nobody to spawn says so once** (`[NpcWorldSim] '<id>' should spawn but …`): an empty plan, or no planned member with a prefab, used to leave the group folded beside a player in silence. A missing stand-in prefab is also warned by the director (`no stand-in prefab`).
- **`STAND_INS=0` in `expedition-host` with everything else green was the observer, not the band** (2026-10-04): the host's player was still strapped into the crash-landed ship, whose seat undid every move (Testing.md Gotchas), so nobody came within the sim's 250 m spawn radius of the folded band; the walk-in still spawned it because the hand-off point lies ~214 m from the ship at the spawn point.
- **Deaths come from `OnDeath`, never from "not read back".** A chunk unload destroys a stand-in with no fold and no `ReadBack`; `ReadStandIns` forgets it, and it respawns with its last health. A non-war group is never `WipedOut`.
- **A lent kit weapon used to be saved into the resident's own bag** (a save mid-muster made the spear its own). `ExpeditionMember` implements `ILentSlots`; `EntityInventorySaveable` writes a lent slot empty, positions kept.
- **NetworkVariables are written in the server's `OnNetworkSpawn`** from the plain copy `Stamp` fills (NGO warns on writes before spawn); the presence `Stamp` publishes (`Activity.Expedition`) reaches clients the same way, from `ResidentPresence.OnNetworkSpawn`.
- **A dead member with no resident in the scene no longer loses its death silently.** `ApplyFinished` used to retire the band anyway. Now it warns (band, `residentKey`) and keeps the record while the world's baked catalog roster still lists the key, so a later load that finds the body applies the death; a key the catalog does not list is a resident that is gone, and the band is retired. The catalog roster, not the reported one: `ReportRoster` runs first in the same pass and lists only residents found alive, so it can never list a missing body. A kept record keeps that key spoken for (`CollectSpokenFor`) until the catalog is re-baked.
- **A stand-in has no `Settlement`** (`Resident.Settlement` is null for `IsStandIn`), so routine and planner stand down. It also never gets `QuietStockModules`/`ApplyDerivedTuning` (they need a society): it keeps the prefab's stock chatter, look-around and temperament, and cannot speak (the voice table is the society's). Phase 2.
- **`FormationModule` ships disabled on resident prefabs** (Prepare Resident Prefabs): enabled, it registers under the default id `"caravan"` and puts every resident at home in one formation. `Stamp` sets the band id and enables it.
- **The kit IS the stand-in's bag** (weapon, tool, belt items), packed before `ResidentCarry.Start`, which fills only an empty bag; a kit over the bag size (4) is truncated with a warning.
- **The director knows a settlement only from the baked catalog** (`Seed` in `Awake`, from `WorldStreamer.Config.siteCatalog`). One the catalog lacks gets a state when its chunk loads (`ReportRoster`), but `TryRaise` refuses it once a day ("not in this world's site catalog"). A settlement without `SettlementExpeditions`, or a world without a director, does nothing and logs nothing.
- **No chunk scene holds a `WorldSiteMarker` today**, so every Travel stage goes to a seeded ring point on open ground; `travelSiteKinds` only matters once sites are placed and baked.
- **The muster is performed, not planned.** An Assembly place holds one resident, so the performer fans the band out with `MusterRow` itself, and a `Scripted` override has no facing, so the row does not turn to face out. The departure line comes from the culture's line table; the profile has no lines of its own (its unread `lines` field was removed 2026-10-04).
- **Unity dropped two new files from its compile list** while this was written (`ExpeditionRecord.cs`, `ExpeditionContentAuthoring.cs`); renaming the file (and class) was the fix — see [Testing.md](Testing.md) for the `typecheck.py` side.
- Smaller traps: a resident that dies at home before the hand-off still gets a stand-in; hooks are null on a restored band until `AdoptGroups`; a posed spawn is still NavMesh-sampled (may lift ~0.26 m); a spawned band with an unreachable Travel target can stall (no Travel time limit — `/exp advance`); finished bands with dead pile up for a settlement never loaded again; `ReportRoster` never re-plans a band already raised; `ExpeditionCatalog.Instance` caches an empty catalog if read before the asset exists (until the next domain reload); `/exp force` ignores the two-band bound.
- **Verified 2026-10-04:** two host play tests (Editor, disposable world) and the `expedition-host` / `-client` / `-persist` autotest modes ([Testing.md](Testing.md)) all pass. Not yet judged by eye: whether the in-place swap shows a pop on a client.

## Extending
**Make another settlement run bands** — nothing in code changes:
1. The settlement has residents: its `SettlementConfig.culture` is set (`AstronautSettlement.asset` has none today).
2. **Culture content** (culture-specific, outside the genericity scan): an `ExpeditionProfile` with goals and kits, `culture` ↔ `SettlementCulture.expeditions` both ways, appended to `ExpeditionCatalog.profiles`, and `expeditionRoles` on the culture's archetypes. For the nomads: `Tools/SpaceGame/Expeditions/Author Expedition Content` (`ExpeditionContentMenu`, idempotent, owns `Goal_Scout`, `Kit_*`, `Muster.asset`); copy it for a new culture.
3. **Stamp Settlement Identity** — an authored `SaveableEntity` (`External`) on every chunk-scene `Settlement`, plus `SettlementExpeditions` where the culture has a profile; on the scene instance, never the prefab.
4. **Prepare Resident Prefabs** — `ExpeditionMember` + a disabled `FormationModule` (Social priority) on every `culture.profiles` prefab **and** every `characters`/`specialCharacters` prefab of every `SettlementConfig` whose culture has a profile (a body made for one role has no culture profile); then Sync Network Prefabs → Wire Saveable Prefabs → Ragdoll Wire Prefabs. Re-run after adding characters to such a config.
5. **Quotas at Generate (a regenerate keeps working):** `Settlement.Populate` moves in first, quota by quota (warriors, then `roleQuotas`), the rolled copies **certain** to have the role (`ExpeditionRules.PlanMoveIn`; candidates are copies whose role has a place here, `ResidentAssignment.Places.CanHost`): a body made for an archetype with the role (`ResidentAssignment.RoleOf`) first, then a body whose every archetype it can be dealt has the role (`ResidentAssignment.CertainOneOf`); the rest fill the beds in the same seeded order as before, and the move-in keeps pool order. The summary line prints `quotas: Warrior 13/12, Scout 5/3` (residents now holding the role / quota) and warns when one is short; `ResidentValidator` reports a short quota as an Error. A config that lists enough bodies made for the roles (`RaxySettlement.asset`, Gotchas) needs no recast.
