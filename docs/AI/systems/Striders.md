# Striders

The third tribe on the faction stack, whose home is not a place but a **walking city**: an `NpcWorldSim` caravan of three crewed RigWalker houses, three crewed dune barges, two digging DesertCrawler workers, eight monowheel scouts and two crab outriders that walks between salvage sites, puts its crew ashore at each stop and takes them back aboard before it moves on, and keeps two of its scouts out on a sweep round it (`ScoutRota`). Its war parties are monowheel convoys. Built on the Sky Tribe's pattern ([SkyTribe.md](SkyTribe.md)); the generic mechanisms — factions, `NpcWorldSim`, `FormationModule`, `NpcTaskModule`, seating — are owned by [AgentSystem.md](AgentSystem.md) and [Vehicles.md](Vehicles.md). Spec: [2026-09-23-striders-walking-city-design.md](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-23-striders-walking-city-design.md).

## Model

- **Faction.** `StriderFaction.asset` ("The Striders", Neutral, HUD rust red, no row in `GlobalRelationships.asset`), listed in `FactionGoodwillLedger.tribes`. Authored by `RosterAuthoring.AuthorStriderFaction`, which shares `AuthorTribeFaction` with the Sky Tribe.
- **People.** `Strider_{Horned,Longcoat,Warrior,Beanie}` — the user's own four models (`strider1.blend`'s `Collection`, `Collection 3`, `warrior`, `Collection 4`, rigged in `strider_characters.blend`: [ArtPipeline.md](ArtPipeline.md)), one skinned mesh each on a Humanoid avatar, in their authored palette colours (`NomadRecipe.ClothPalette` null: no recolour, no wind cloth), scaled to 3.0 m by `NomadPrefabBuilder`. They took over the four dyed-nomad prefabs by `AssetDatabase.MoveAsset` (Umber → Horned, Tan → Longcoat, Maroon → Warrior, StrawHat → Beanie), so their GUIDs — and every reference from the roster, the monowheels, the crab, the template, saves and the network prefab list — are unchanged; `NomadPrefabBuilder.StriderNomads` keeps that order (index 0 rides the crab and the monowheels, index 1 gunners). `NomadRecipe.TravelsToGoals` adds a `GoalTravelModule` (priority `Fallback + 1`) so crew ashore can be walked to and from their gangway. **Elders.** `StriderElder` (`StriderElderBuilder`) — the user's cyborg, a Strider's torso, head and arms on four robotic walker legs, rigged as a rigid-part walker ([ArtPipeline.md](ArtPipeline.md)) and built on the crab's stack (`CrabWalkerBuilder.BuildBodyFrom`: `CrabLocomotion` with four `Coxa_/Hip_/Knee_/Ankle_/Foot_<FL|FR|RL|RR>` chains measured from their pins, `CrabDriver` with `lateralSteering` off, `moveSpeed` 1.6, steps 0.3 s — the legs cap it at 0.75 m/s —, `slopeFollow` 0.5), scaled to 3.0 m. Health 200, faction, `GoalTravelModule`; no perception or targeting (a target it cannot fight would count as a fight for `CrewShift` and pause its recall clock for good), no attack, no `WanderModule`, no formation. `StriderElderSway` rolls its chest with the gait phase and leans it with speed, the head held against it (no clips: humanoid clips cannot drive it). `StandingRider` makes it a standing-post crew member. Not in the roster; only the city fields it.
- **Crab outrider.** `StriderCrabOutrider` — a `CrabWalker6` body (`CrabWalkerBuilder.BuildBody`) with the outrider brain of `RobotHorseBuilder.AddOutriderBehaviour`, health 180, mass 100, `UnderTerrainGuard`, `FormationModule` (`holdSlotAtRest` on — it flanks the column and never parks among the lead house's legs; `regroupDistance` 150, the crawler's, since its slot is ~90 m behind the lead house), and an `NpcPassenger` that seats `Strider_Horned`. The mount carries, the rider shoots. The wild `CrabWalker6.prefab` is untouched.
- **Roster.** `Rosters/Striders.asset`: the four nomads as Scout and Warrior, and the five `StriderMonowheel_*` as Rider, all weight 1 and **dealt from a deck** (`FactionRoster.Deal`, [AgentSystem.md](AgentSystem.md)): every round of five deals each wheel once, so no wheel rides twice in a party before every one has ridden (the user, 2026-10-05: "all kinds of monowheels should be used", some were too rare); hand items `GravelBlaster`, `NetGun`, `basicgun`; 8 `StriderHostileLines`. Tiers: 0 = 2 Riders, 1 = 3 Riders, 2 = 5 Riders: 2 and 3 different wheels, then all five — so tier 2 always brings both doubles (5 drivers + 6 gunners), and a tier-0 party a double 7 times in 10 (it was ~1 in 3 at the old 3:1 weights): a harder early party, by the user's call; the weights stay the knob (a lighter wheel comes later in each round). The crab outrider is **not** in the roster: it is the city's flank escort only. The war party (`strider-war-party`) is a **convoy**: every member a monowheel with its driver (and a double's gunners); drivers dismount to fight as every rider does. Folded it travels at `StriderMonowheelBuilder.CruiseSpeed` (14 m/s, written by `WireWorldSim`). It never comes out of the city.
- **Houses.** `StriderHabitatWalker.prefab`, a prefab **variant** of the player's `RigWalker` (catalogue row in [Vehicles.md](Vehicles.md)): helm and `WanderModule` removed; `EntityFaction` (`EntityFactionWiring.Ensure`), `NpcTaskModule` (`travelTimeout` = `StriderCityBuilder.CityTravelTimeout`, ~1389 s), `GoalTravelModule`, `FormationModule` (`holdSlotAtRest` on, restRadius 45, slotTolerance 8, regroup 120), six crew posts (`VesselSeats` + `ChairPose`, one per `StriderCityBuilder.PostLayout` entry) and one **standing post** after them (`StandingPost_0`, front centre of the deck ring between the two front crew posts, raised off the deck by `StriderElderBuilder.StandingHeight()` because the elder's root rides on its hip plane; `CrewShift.standingPosts` = `StriderCityBuilder.StandingPosts` 1), a ground-level `Gangway` and a `CrewShift`. No `HealthComponent` — indestructible.
- **Barges.** `StriderDuneBarge`, `StriderDuneBargeLookout`, `StriderDuneBargeCompact` — prefab **variants** of the three dune barges ([DuneBarge.md](DuneBarge.md)), built by `StriderBargeBuilder` (catalogue row in [Vehicles.md](Vehicles.md)). The agent brain drives each: `AgentController` hands `FormationModule`'s intents to a `TrackedHullMotor` (cruise 4 m/s, turns on the spot; ride height 1.16 m and footprint measured from the track links), and `NetAuthority` keeps both on the server. `FormationModule` (Social, `holdSlotAtRest`, restRadius 45, slotTolerance 4, `regroupDistance` = `CityFarthestSlot` + 30), Strider faction, four crew posts at the corners of the open roof (the largest flat patch of the hull's top, mapped by downward rays, outside every `InteriorReveal` room), a ground-level gangway 4 m off the +x side and a `CrewShift` — the crew half shared with the houses through `CrewDeckWiring`. No helm, no `HealthComponent` (indestructible, never a fighter, no ragdoll), no `ScoutRota` or `NpcTaskModule` (a barge never leads). Players can still walk aboard and use the doors and hatches. `TrackContacts/TrackContact_<Main|Pod>_<L|R>_<Front|Rear>` mark where each track's ground run starts and ends (8 on the full and lookout barges, 4 on the compact, all at ground level); each throws track dust, and each track's pair drives its `TrackBelts` belt: the links circulate and the wheels turn at that side's measured speed ([TrackBelts.md](TrackBelts.md)).
- **Workers.** The shared `DesertCrawler.prefab` is now a Strider (`EntityFactionWiring` and its builder) with a `FormationModule` whose `restRadius` is `DesertCrawlerBuilder.WorkRadius` (60 m): at a stop it roams that ring with its `WanderModule` and `CrawlerToolModule` digs whenever it pauses. Uncrewed, no health. The lone placed crawler in `Chunk_7_4` became a Strider worker with it.
- **Dust.** The houses, crawlers and crab outriders throw sand from every foot that lands, the barges churn it at their track contacts: the monowheel's lingering cloud, wired by each builder through `VehicleDustWiring`. Components and the city's particle budget (2 448 puffs at most, ~5 170 with the scouts' monowheels): [VehicleDust.md](VehicleDust.md).
- **The city.** `strider-city`, a seeded (`runtimeOnly: 0`) `NpcGroupTemplate` in `persistentScene.unity`, written by `RosterAuthoring.WireStriderCity`: 1 lead house (`isLeader`), then its 16 followers, listed by kind and **dealt into a new order in every world** (`ColumnDeal`, below): 1 house (two in all: three read as too many, the user, 2026-10-05), 2 crawlers, 2 crabs (the fixed `StriderCrabOutrider` prefab, role Rider — never the roster's Rider draw, which is the monowheels), the barges `StriderDuneBarge`, `_Lookout` and `_Compact`, and the scouts, every kind of monowheel: `StriderMonowheel_Runner`, `_Hauler`, `_Patched` (2 each), `_Double` and `_DoubleWide` (1 each, with their 3 gunners) — `StriderCityScouts` (8) fixed prefabs, role Scout, shared by `ScoutShare` (the doubles joined 2026-10-05: they never rode with the city) — then 12 Warrior + 12 Scout **crew** (6 per house, 4 per barge; `crew: true`, one per post; an odd total gives the extra post to a Scout), and last the **elders** (`StriderElder`, `crew: true`, count drawn by `countWeights` = `RosterAuthoring.StriderCityElders` {1: 0.75, 2: 0.25} (at most one per house) from the group's `RosterSeed`, so a reload brings back the same number, and the seed is the new world's own (`NpcWorldSim.NewWorldSeed`, saved in `Record.rosterSeed`), so each world draws its own; last because a drawn count shifts every later member's plan index). They take the houses' standing posts lead house first (`CrewShift.FirstWithRoom(Live, prefab)`), at most one per house. Followers take slots in spawn order, so the order *is* the column. Listed by kind it marched as a parade of matching pairs (the user wanted it mixed, 2026-10-04); shuffled once at authoring time it was the same column in every world (the user, 2026-10-05: "like drawing from a card deck", "randomness and chaos"). So every follower spec carries a `ColumnCard` (`column`: `shuffled`, `kind`, `withinFirstSlots`, `keepsClear`, `footprint`, `slotTolerance`), written by `WireStriderCity` from `StriderCityColumn.Card` (footprints measured from the prefabs' renderers, so the server never loads a prefab to deal), and `NpcGroupComposition.Resolve` deals them from the group's `RosterSeed` (`ColumnDeal.TryDeal`): slot by slot the next card is drawn from what is left of the shuffled deck, a card that breaks a rule there goes back, and a column that runs dry starts over. The rules: every carrier (house or barge) rides in the first `CityCarrierSlots` (6 rows × 2 lanes = 12, the level footprint), no house, crawler, crab or barge sits beside its own kind in a row or nose to tail behind it in a lane (`CityKind`; every monowheel is kindless and goes anywhere — spacing out 8 of 17 would itself be a pattern), and every barge clears its neighbours (`keepsClear`, Gotchas). The leader is always the flagged house; the crew and elders always come after the vehicles. Starts (`useStartPosition`) **where the user stood in play and asked for it** (2026-10-05), at the point `StriderCityStartSite` picks, (3368, 106, 1199): of a 50 m grid (`CityStartStep`) within `CityStartReach` (300 m) of `CityStartAnchor` (3418, 1199), listed nearest first, the first whose terrain point is owed ground and on the baked world NavMesh and whose footprint is level by `CityLevelGround` over the chunk heightmaps (15.3 m of relief, limit 22.9); none -> an error and no start written. It was anchored on the `SpawnPoint` until the merge with main moved that 1.2 km away (the ship now lands at ~(2954, 557)), so the city is no longer beside the landing spot. The anchor is ~450 m from the Clanker town, so the back of the column can reach its alarm ring. Left to a Ruin it seeded at the sim origin ~4 km off, because no Ruin is registered at seed time. It then **stays parked there for `initialStaySeconds` = `RosterAuthoring.StriderCityInitialStay` (600 s)** — seeded as the record's `DwellRemaining`, handed to the lead house if it unfolds before then — so a new player can walk over before it marches 1–2.5 km to its first stop. Only new worlds use either; an existing save keeps its record wherever the city walked. In a dev play session `Tools/SpaceGame/Dev/Teleport to Strider City` dismounts the local player, preloads the ground 150 m from the city and lands there, so the city unfolds (spawnRadius 250 m). Stops: Ruin / ScrapField, search radius `StriderCityBuilder.CitySearchRadius` 2500, held to level ground by every task's `levelGround` = `StriderCityBuilder.CityLevelGround` (Flows), dwell 90–180 s, arrive radius 25 (roam points only — a registered site's own radius decides arrival, Gotchas), travel multiplier 0.45. Formation `RosterAuthoring.CityShape`: Lanes 2, lanes 30 m, rows 35 m (for the barges, Gotchas); `CityFarthestSlot` (`FormationMath.FarthestSlot` over the `CityFollowers` 16 slots, jitter and drift included, ≈288 m) is what every member's `regroupDistance` must clear: all five city builders write `RosterAuthoring.CityRegroupDistance` (`CityFarthestSlot` + 30 m ≈ 318 m), since any vehicle may draw the last slot. `travelSpeed` = `StriderCityBuilder.CityLeaderSpeed` (2.7 m/s). No vessels; the rest of its `transport` block (28 m/s, "Sky City", dock spacing) is an inert leftover of the copied `sand-nomads` template. It is `showFromAfar`: folded, it is drawn from afar on every machine ([SettlementLods.md](SettlementLods.md)).

## Flows

**Author + build order** (each step idempotent; re-run the whole chain after a recipe change):
1. `Tools/SpaceGame/Agents/Author Strider Faction`
2. `Tools/SpaceGame/Agents/Build Strider Nomad NPCs` — pass 1, unarmed ("No roster at …" is expected)
3. `Tools/Creatures/Build Strider Crab Outrider`, then `Tools/Creatures/Build Strider Elder` (before the habitat: its standing post is raised by the elder's hip height)
4. `Tools/SpaceGame/Agents/Author Strider Roster` — validation reads the prefabs' baked faction; its Riders are the monowheels, so on a fresh checkout run step 11 first (then re-run this)
5. `Build Strider Nomad NPCs` again — bakes `roster.handItems` into `NpcRandomLoadout`
6. `Build Strider Crab Outrider` again — its rider is the rebuilt nomad
7. `Tools/Vehicles/Build Desert Crawler Prefab`
8. `Tools/SpaceGame/Vehicles/Build Strider Habitat Walker`, then `Tools/SpaceGame/Vehicles/Build Strider Barges` (needs the dune barges, `Tools/Vehicles/Build Dune Barge Prefabs`). Every city vehicle builder (steps 3, 6, 7, 8, 11) adds its near dust, then far dust ([VehicleDust.md](VehicleDust.md)) and the bake ([SettlementLods.md](SettlementLods.md)) right before saving
9. `Tools/SpaceGame/Agents/Wire War Party Templates` (adds/updates `strider-war-party`)
10. `Tools/SpaceGame/Agents/Wire Strider City` (needs the faction, the habitat, the barges, the crawler, the crab and all five monowheels; touches no other template). Re-run it after rebuilding any city vehicle: each card's footprint and `slotTolerance` are measured from its prefab. After a change to the column re-run step 11 and `Build Strider Barges`: both derive `regroupDistance` from it
11. `Tools/SpaceGame/Vehicles/Build Strider Monowheels` (needs the Strider nomads and the parallel session's `Monowheel_<V>` art prefabs — `Tools/Vehicles/Build Monowheel Presentation`). **Re-run it after every presentation rebuild**: the seat markers are added children of the nested art, and the colliders are measured from it

**Spawn.** Inside `spawnRadius`, `NpcWorldSim.Spawn` walks the plan in order (places from `GroupColumnLayout.Places`, shared with the distant silhouette): the lead house, the vehicles in the order `ColumnDeal` dealt them from the group's seed (the same order on every unfold and reload of that world), then the crew and elders. The vehicles take formation slots in that order (the scouts ride theirs as the fast escort, `holdSlotAtRest` at a stop). A crew member asks `CrewShift.FirstWithRoom(group.Live)` for a house already spawned; with `CrewAshore` false it spawns `seated` (agent off, `NpcSpawn.Create(seated: true)`) at the house and is `Take(member, aboard: true)`-n onto a post via `VesselSeats.Seat`; with `CrewAshore` true it spawns on foot at `GangwayPoint` and is `Take(…, aboard: false)`-n, which marks the house `Ashore`. Crew never count as vessel riders (`NpcGroupComposition.Rides` excludes them), so a crewed flying template sizes its vessel by its other members. No room anywhere → warning, the member walks.

**The crew cycle** (`CrewShift.Update`, every server frame; `atStop` = the leader's `NpcTaskModule.AtStop`):

| State | Does | Leaves when |
|---|---|---|
| Aboard | Crew on posts; they keep their own targeting and fire from the deck, seeing and shooting through their own house's walls (perception skips a seated NPC's carrier, [AgentSystem.md](AgentSystem.md)). Their `AgentGroundConform` is parked on every machine while seated ([AgentSystem.md](AgentSystem.md)) | the leader is at a stop and anyone lives → Disembarking |
| Disembarking | Unseats one crew member every `disembarkInterval` at `GangwayPoint` | nobody left seated → Ashore; the stop ends first → Recalling |
| Ashore | Crew wander; anyone beyond `ashoreRadius` of the gangway is walked back to half of it | the stop ends → Recalling |
| Recalling | Each crew member walks to the gangway and is seated within `boardRadius`; after `recallTimeout` (clock paused while anyone fights) stragglers are seated where they stand | every living member seated → Aboard |

**Leaving.** The house `FormationModule.LeaderOf(formationId)` names installs `() => CrewShift.AllAboard(id)` as its own `NpcTaskModule` departure gate (re-checked every tick; a house that stops leading takes it down). So a stay can end but the column does not choose its next stop until every house is Aboard. The gate waits for the houses' crew only: the crawlers (3.2 m/s), crabs (3.2 m/s) and scouts (up to 20 m/s) are faster than the leader's 2.7, so they catch up with their slots; one farther than its `regroupDistance` from the leader rides straight for the leader, then back to its slot. Dead crew never block — they drop out of the census.

**Choosing a stop** (server: the live leader's `NpcTaskModule`, or the folded record's `NpcWorldSim.ChooseTask` — both call `NpcTaskPlanner.ResolveDestination` with the template's task, so both apply its `levelGround`). A registered Ruin/ScrapField is kept only if `TryLevelStop` finds level ground within 200 m of it (the stop is then that level spot); otherwise `TryLevelRoamPoint` draws up to 8 ordinary roam points (`TryRoamPoint`, 825–2500 m out) and nudges each onto level ground with `LevelGroundSearch` (rings 100 m apart out to 200 m; the first spot whose nine footprint samples — centre, mid-edges and corners of the 435 m square — span ≤ 22.8 m). Samples come from the world NavMesh (`NavMeshGround.TryHeight`, probed from the candidate's height, reach 30 m, ≤ 10 m sideways): a sample in a hole (rock, building), off a ledge or off the mesh leaves the footprint incomplete, which rejects the spot. Nothing level → the live leader waits `retryDelay` (5 s) and chooses again; the folded record falls back to an unfiltered roam point (Gotchas). The stop is where the leader halts; the houses hold their slots on the level square and the crew go ashore at their gangways.

**Scouting** (`ScoutRota.Tick`, server, the lead house only). Every frame it rides its outings: a scout that reaches its waypoint gets the next one (all `sweepPoints`, then the first again to close the ring), placed round where the house is *now* (the ring follows the moving city; `TrySetSampled` onto the NavMesh, else the raw point) and kept on streamed-in ground: a waypoint whose chunk is not `Loaded` (`WorldStreamer.IsChunkLoadedAt`, server-side chunk state) is pulled in toward the house `pullInStep` (100 m) at a time (`ScoutRotaLogic.TryPullIn`), skipped when nothing is loaded down to `sweepArriveRadius`, and with no waypoint left the sweep ends (a scout is not sent at all when none is reachable); no chunk is pinned; a sweep ends when the loop is ridden or the timeout passes, and the scout turns for home but still counts as away until it is within its `regroupDistance` of the leader; a scout that loses its rider (dead, knocked off, a player took the wheel), is parked (`enabled` off by the gate or `MountModule`), or whose goal is no longer the rota's is given back at once and not counted. Every `rotaInterval`, unless enough are away, it lists the formation's monowheels (`FormationModule.CollectMembers`) and sends `PickNext`'s choice, never a scout whose rider has a target (the spec's "a fighting scout finishes its fight first" is implemented as *a fighting scout is not sent*; a scout already sweeping whose rider picks a target keeps riding the loop); a pair sent together starts on opposite sides of the ring. **Going out:** `FormationModule.SetRuntimeActive(false)` + `AgentGoal.Set(waypoint)` (no multiplier: the wheel's `GoalTravelModule` already rides goals at cruise); `GoalTravelModule` drives. **Coming home:** `SetRuntimeActive(true)` + clear the goal (only if it is still the rota's); past `regroupDistance` (≈247) formation rides it straight to the leader, then to its slot. A house that stops leading, or is disabled, calls all its scouts home first.

**Convoy** (`strider-war-party`). Its formation is `RosterAuthoring.ConvoyShape` (Lanes 2, rows 18 m, lanes 14 m, jitter 1.5/2, drift 1 at 0.1), written by `WireWorldSim` over the infantry file (rows 3.5 m) the copied Sand template carries; `StriderWarPartyTemplateTests` measures every wheel's collider footprint (≤ 8.9 m long, ≤ 5.7 m wide) against it. `NpcGroupComposition.Resolve` draws each tier's Riders from the roster; the first drawn leads, and every monowheel spawns its driver (`NpcPassenger`) and, on a double, 3 gunners (`MountedGunners`), all stamped into the party. A driver hurt or killed dismounts and fights on foot, and the wheel left riderless is the group's no longer (below); gunners stay seated and fire until the wheel dies, when `MonowheelWreck` stands its living driver (`NpcPassenger.Dismount`) and a double's gunners (`ReleaseGunners`) beside it. Everybody on foot is one of the party's `Fighters` and folds with it. A wheel whose driver is lost is parked by `MonowheelDriverGate`, which disables its `FormationModule`, so `FormationModule.LeaderOf` hands the lead to the next wheel; the world sim steers that **effective** leader (`NpcWorldSim.SteeringLeader`, `LeadsFormation`): `SteerSpawned` sets the director's trail on it, and `HandGoalToSteeringLeader` (every spawned tick) gives a handed-on leader with no goal the group's goal, so the party keeps coming. **A player who mounts a party's wheel takes it** (`GroupMembership` hears the server's `MountModule.Mounted` and calls `NpcWorldSim.ReleaseToPlayer`): out of `Live`/`Fighters`, out of the column (`FormationModule.LeaveFormation`), goal cleared, its seats stood down (`ICrewedSeats`: the saddle stays empty, its driver already vacated by the mount, and a double's gunners step off and fight on as the party's), and it becomes an ordinary saved player vehicle. **A wheel the party loses is left behind** (`AbandonedVehicle`): the moment its driver is off or it is wrecked, `NpcWorldSim.ReleaseDefeated` releases it exactly as `ReleaseToPlayer` does — so a double's gunners step off then and fight on foot — and it stays where it stopped for 300 s, through the party's fold or disband, then goes once no player is within 150 m. A player who mounts a driverless one before then keeps it for good; a wreck's saddle is off, so it cannot be claimed.

**Fold.** `ReadBackCrew` runs first: `CrewAshore = AnyAshore(Live)`, and while the leader's `NpcTaskModule.AtStop` it copies `PhaseTimer` into `DwellRemaining` and clears `HasGoal` (as a virtual arrival does). Then `DespawnMembers` destroys `Live` in reverse spawn order — crew before the houses they are parented to. Folded, `TickVirtual` keeps `CrewAshore` while `DwellRemaining` lasts and clears it once the stay has run out (the point it leaves its stop), so it unfolds on foot mid-stop and marching with its crew seated after.

## Multiplayer

Every decision is server-side: `NpcWorldSim` (`Network.Decides`), `CrewShift.Update` and `ScoutRota.Update` (`Network.Simulates`; a scout's ride out replicates as ordinary agent movement, so the rota sends no message). Seating needs no message of its own: `VesselSeats.Seat` → `NpcSeating` parents a seated crew member under the house's `NetworkObject`, as for sky-vessel riders. An elder on its standing post is parented the same way; `StandingRider` parks and resumes its legs from that parenting on every machine, and its sway runs from the replicated body. The Strider people, crab, elder, habitat and crawler are registered network prefabs (`NetworkPrefabRegistrar.Sync(out _, out _)` at the end of each builder), all with `SceneMigrationSynchronization` on. Players can stand on a house deck (`WalkerPlatformCarrier`) but cannot take a helm — there is none. **Not yet verified on a client** (Task 15): seated crew pose, step-off/re-board, crab riders, a late joiner mid-stop, the chunk migration of a spawned Strider.

## Persistence

Faction, roster, prefabs and the template are authored assets. The city's members are never saved individually (`NpcSpawn.Create` disowns them); the `strider-city` group record decides, and `Record.crewAshore` (appended 2026-09-24; an older save reads `false`, i.e. marching with crew seated) says whether the crew come back seated or on foot at their gangways. `CaptureRecords` calls `ReadBackCrew` for each spawned group before it writes, so `Record.dwellRemaining` carries the live leader's remaining stay too. `CrewShift` holds no saved state: every crew member is re-`Take`n on every spawn. **Not yet verified by a reload** (Task 15): mid-march, mid-stop, and an older save with no `strider-city` record.

The column order is never saved either: `ColumnDeal` deals it from `Record.rosterSeed`, so a refold or a reload of a world brings back the column it had, and a new world (`NewWorldSeed`) deals its own; `NpcGroupCompositionTests.ShuffledMembers_ARestoredGroup_DealsTheSameColumn` restores a group from its record and compares. A world saved before 2026-10-05 keeps its saved seed and deals a column from it at its next spawn — the column it showed (the authored, sorted one) is gone, with nothing lost. Elders are group members like the crew: never saved individually; their number comes back from the seed and their aboard/ashore state with `Record.crewAshore`. **Not yet verified in play** (host, client, reload). `ScoutRota` saves nothing, by design (spec §3): scouts are group members, spawned from their prefabs with formation on and no goal, so after a reload or an unfold every scout is in the column, a sweep in progress is forgotten, and the rota (fresh `HomeFor` for all) sends the first two listed on its first tick. `MountedGunners` saves nothing either: gunners are `NpcSpawn`-disowned and re-spawned into every empty seat when the double spawns, so a standalone double comes back fully crewed after a load — gunner deaths are not saved.

**A wheel a player took is the world's** (closes spec §6's follow-up). `NpcWorldSim.ReleaseToPlayer` runs `SaveablePolicy.EnsureSpawned` (runtime savers, `MarkIdentityFinal`) and `SaveableEntity.ReclaimForWorld()` (the inverse of `DisownToExternal`), so the world store captures it by its prefab id like any runtime vehicle (`MountSaveable` puts a saved rider back). The stand-down of its seats is saved by `CrewSaveable` (key `crew`, added by `SaveablePolicy.Ensure` for any `ICrewedSeats`, captured only when stood down): a load network-spawns the record *before* restoring it, so a double's `OnNetworkSpawn` has already seated fresh gunners, which the restore despawns, while the saddle's rider waits for `Start` and never spawns. **Not yet verified by a reload in play.** **A wheel the party lost is the world's too, for a while.** `ReleaseDefeated` is the same release, so a left-behind wheel is in the world store with its seats stood down; `AbandonedVehicleSaveable` (key `abandoned`, `{ remaining }`, captured only while `Abandoned`) puts its countdown back, so a save/quit/load — or its chunk unloading, since a released wheel pins no chunk — pauses the 300 s rather than restarting or losing it. A wreck comes back dead and stays (corpse despawn is off; `MonowheelWreck` re-stops it from the restored `OnDeath`). It is never a group member again, so a reload cannot duplicate it into its party: the party's record spawns fresh wheels, and a load rebuilds runtime groups from records only. A claimed wheel saves nothing under `abandoned` and loads `Kept`. Pinned by `AbandonedVehicleTests` (EditMode); **not yet verified by a reload in play.**

## Gotchas

- **`LeggedDriver` never drives faster than 1× its `moveSpeed`,** so a follower machine keeps up only with a leader slower than itself. The leader walks at `CityTravelMultiplier` 0.45 × the RigWalker's 6 m/s = 2.7 m/s (measured 2.65–2.95 with speed variation); the crawler's 3.2 leaves thin headroom — in the spike it lost 18 m on a turn and took ~40 s to close. Never raise the multiplier. The RigWalker's gait tops out at 5.64 m/s, not 6.
- **The crab's gait, not its `moveSpeed`, caps it**: stock `CrabLocomotion.stepDuration` 0.4 gives `MaxSpeed` 1.76 m/s whatever `CrabDriver.moveSpeed` says. The outrider sets `stepDuration` 0.22 → 3.2 m/s with feet still planted; `swingLegs` 3 is faster but hops with no feet down. The footfall is 1.8× the authored cadence (not yet eyeballed).
- **Nothing parks.** With the default rest behaviour a follower settles anywhere inside `restRadius` of its rest point — two houses stopped 17–19 m apart with 8 collider pairs clipping. Houses carry `holdSlotAtRest` (and no `WanderModule`) so they stay on marching slots ≥30 m apart; so do the crab outriders, whose default 6 m rest ring would put them among the lead house's legs. Crawlers deliberately keep the rest ring so they spread out and dig.
- **A legged rider's legs must be parked on a post, not just its motor.** `NpcSeating` switches off motors, but a `LeggedLocomotion` owns the body and writes it from its own path every `LateUpdate`, so on a moving deck it drags the elder back to where it boarded, and when unseated it would walk back there. `StandingRider` disables the locomotion while carried and resumes it from the transform (`ResumeFromCarry`), at order 90 so the legs never get a frame to write a stale path. **Shipped elder, known defect (2026-10-05):** `strider_elder.fbx` was rigged before `strider_elder_rig.py` took the chest joint from the chest segment — it took the first chest-bound segment, an arm — so its chest sways about a point in its left arm, and its foot pins hang 1.8 cm under the soles (the standing post floats it ~3 cm). The script is fixed and not yet re-run (the machine never had the memory): re-run `batch` = copy `strider1.blend` → rig the four humanoids → `strider_elder_rig.py` → export the elder → `Build Strider Elder` → `Build Strider Habitat Walker`.
- **List crew after their carriers.** `FirstWithRoom` only sees members already in `Live`; a template that lists crew first spawns them on foot with a "more crew than crew posts" warning.
- **`Take(member, aboard: true)` only after both are network-spawned** — `NpcSpawn.Create` spawns before it returns, and carriers precede crew, so `Spawn` satisfies it. Seat earlier and `NpcSeating` falls back to local parenting that clients never see. A failed seat logs `[CrewShift] Could not seat…` and leaves the member unlisted (and, spawned seated, frozen with its agent off).
- **Despawn crew before their carrier.** Seated crew are children of the house's `NetworkObject`; despawn the house first and netcode lifts them to the scene root with nothing to release them. `DespawnOrder` reverses `Live`; keep carriers before crew in the template or the reversal is wrong.
- **`ReadBackCrew` before any fold or save** — after `DespawnMembers` `Live` is empty and `AnyAshore` reads false, so the crew would come back seated mid-stop. It must also carry the live leader's remaining stay: `DwellRemaining` is otherwise set only by a virtual arrival, so it is usually spent, and `TickVirtual` (which runs before the spawn check) would clear `CrewAshore` on the first folded tick. `RuntimeGroupTests.ACityFoldedMidStop_KeepsItsCrewAshore_ForTheRestOfTheLeadersStay` pins it. A stay already over but held by the gate folds with a spent dwell, and the record takes its crew aboard at once — the stop was ending anyway.
- **The gate lives on whichever house `LeaderOf` names, re-checked per tick** — `LeaderOf` falls back to the first live member (registration order = spawn order) when no flagged leader is active. The leader must be a house: a crawler or crab has no `CrewShift`, so nobody would hold the gate and the column would stop waiting. `WireStriderCity` flags a house and lists the houses first; keep both.
- **The gate is checked in `Choosing` too, not only at the end of a dwell.** A spawned leader starts in `Choosing` (`SetTasks`) unless the record still has a stay left, which `NpcWorldSim.Configure` hands it (`NpcTaskModule.ResumeTask`); a reload at a stop restores `Record.dwellRemaining` the same way, but a stay that ran out while folded leaves it choosing at once — held by the gate only if its crew is ashore. The gate must be up before the leader's first tick, and `Update` order (`CrewShift` vs `AgentController`) does not guarantee that, so `CrewShift.Take` installs the registration and gate itself (`FollowFormation`) — `NpcWorldSim.Configure` has set the house's formation id by the time its crew are taken. `CrewShiftTests.Take_Ashore_ClosesTheLeadersGate_BeforeAnyUpdate` pins it.
- **A double's gunners are lifted to the scene root before any hook on the mount runs.** `NetworkSpawnManager.OnDespawnObject` reparents first-generation child NetworkObjects to the root, *then* calls `OnNetworkDespawn`, and `OnDestroy` comes later still. So `MountedGunners` despawns them in `OnNetworkDespawn` — the earliest hook, and still before the mount's own despawn message goes out — and `OnDestroy` covers a mount that never network-despawned — unnetworked, never spawned, or a session shutdown. In the world sim nothing is lifted at all: a gunner is a group fighter outside `Live` and not an `NpcPassenger` rider, so `DespawnMembers` despawns it before any member; the mount's later `NpcSpawn.Remove` of a despawned gunner is a harmless second `Destroy`.
- **Monowheel seats are upright markers at each seat's back corner, not the art's sockets.** `Socket_Rider_*` / `Socket_Passenger_*` import pitched -90° (Blender empties): seated on one, a rider lies on their back and the offset pushes them forward instead of down. They are not on the cushions either (the Double's rider socket is 0.16 m ahead of its backrest). The builder puts `Seat_Rider` / `Seat_Passenger` where the top of the seat cushion meets the front of the back cushion (`Mesh_SeatCushion_*` / `Mesh_BackCushion_*`, `Mesh_PassengerSeatCushion_*` / `Mesh_PassengerBackCushion_*`, measured from their vertices, not renderer bounds, which re-box the import rotation), and `Seat_Side_{0,1}` on the side cushions' top back edge, with no rotation, as added children of `Body`. They tip with `MonowheelLean`'s pose, and `TiltingSeats` holds every rider on them each frame — without it a rider stayed upright where the seat was when they sat down, because every networked seating path parents to the root ([Vehicles.md](Vehicles.md)).
- **One seat offset cannot seat both the player and a Strider.** The player's origin is a metre above its soles ([PlayerCharacter.md](PlayerCharacter.md)), a Strider's is at its feet, and the robot horse's `(0, -0.95, 0)` drop the wheels carried sank a seated player about 1.4 m into the chassis. `SeatedBodyMeasure.SitCorner` sits each body down in the humanoid `Sit` state in a preview scene, bakes its largest skinned mesh (the body; hanging cloth is separate) and reads the underside below the hips and the back of the pelvis; the offset is minus that corner (player ≈ (0, +0.50, +0.31), a Strider ≈ (0, -0.79, +0.48)). `MountModule` takes the player's, `NpcPassenger`, `VesselSeats` and `TiltingSeats` the Strider's. A new rider rig, or a new Sit clip, needs nothing but a rebuild.
- **Monowheels live under `Prefabs/Agents/Vehicles/`, not `Characters/Striders/`.** `RagdollWiring.IsVehicle` goes by that folder; anywhere else `AgentController` + `HealthComponent` earns a vehicle an `AgentRagdoll` the next time any builder runs the ragdoll pass.
- **A monowheel's ring is a hollow hoop, not a solid sphere.** A sphere the size of the ring (r 1.97) enclosed every rider — a raycast at one hit the wheel — and made the wheel 1.7 m wider than it looks. The wheel rolls on a 0.7 m contact sphere at the bottom of each ring, and thin boxes make the rest of the ring. `MonowheelPresentation`'s ground probe starts 0.6 m above the contact and skips only colliders under `Body`, so the contact sphere must be ≥ 0.6 m (a raycast never reports the collider it starts in) and the bottom hoop segment is left out. `ASeatedRider_IsInTheOpen_SoTheyCanBeShot` pins the seats clear of every collider. A seated rider's feet still dangle into the contact sphere.
- **The hoop must be inscribed in the wheel, never centred on it.** Boxes centred on the paddle circle poke past it at the corners: the two beside the bottom stood 13 cm below the contact, so the vehicle rested on them with its contact sphere in the air (0.13 m on flat ground, 0.2 m on slopes), and an upright hoop round the DoubleWide's 20°-cambered rings reached 0.24 m below their real lowest point. Each segment's outer face is now a chord of the circle, in the ring's plane; `RollsOnAContactSpherePerRing_InsideAHollowHoop` fails any corner past the paddles and `MonowheelGroundContactTests` drops each wheel on flat ground and 10–20° slopes and checks the ring touches.
- **A riderless Strider wheel must not drive itself.** Its `FormationModule`/`GoalTravelModule` stay enabled when its rider dies or is knocked off, and `MountModule` switches them back on when a player steps off; `MonowheelDriverGate` parks them while there is neither an NPC rider nor a mounted player. It records only what it switched off (as `MountModule.RestoreModuleSuppression` does), so the two never fight.
- **A killed monowheel keeps rolling unless stopped.** `HealthReactionModule.ApplyDeadState` only disables the `AgentController`; the `MonowheelMotor` runs on its own `FixedUpdate` and the saddle stays mountable for as long as the wreck lies there. `MonowheelWreck` stops and disables the motor and, on the server only, disables the `MountModule` (which dismounts a rider; a client doing it too would drop its player ahead of the server's replicated dismount). `OnDeath` is also raised on a client through `RestoreHealth` when the server's death replicates: that copy only stops its own motor (`ForceStop` skips the velocity writes on a kinematic remote body). If ownership later returns to the server, `NetAuthority` does not re-enable the motor, because it only restores drivers it disabled itself.
- **The rota parks a scout's formation through the runtime `active` bit (`SetRuntimeActive`), never `enabled`.** `enabled` on a wheel's `FormationModule`/`GoalTravelModule` already has two owners — `MonowheelDriverGate` (no rider) and `MountModule` (a player drives) — each restoring only what it took. A third writer of `enabled` would either be undone by the gate's `Release` or re-enable a module the gate had parked. `SetRuntimeActive` is a separate bit; `ScoutRotaTests` asserts the rota never touches `enabled`. A scout out on a sweep stays registered, so the column keeps a gap in its place instead of reshuffling twice per sweep.
- **The rota lives on the leader, and every house has one.** Leadership moves on an unfold or a reload (`LeaderOf` falls back to the first live member), so it is re-checked per tick like `CrewShift`'s gate; an ex-leader calls its scouts home in the same tick, or they would ride with formation off for ever. The leader must be a house (see the gate Gotcha).
- **A sweep's timeout must outlast the loop.** The closed loop is 600 m out + 6 × 600 m round = 4.2 km: 300 s at the 14 m/s cruise speed the rota sweeps at, i.e. the plan's first 300 s timeout ended almost every sweep early. Close the ring on the first waypoint (`LoopStops`), or the last sixth of it is never swept while `LoopLength` still counts it. `StriderHabitatWalkerTests.CarriesAScoutRota_WhoseTimeoutOutlastsOneLoop` pins it from the asset.
- **A rider knocked off mid-sweep leaves a riderless wheel far out, by design.** The rider dismounts on damage (`NpcPassenger`), the rota drops the scout, and `MonowheelDriverGate` parks the wheel where it stands, up to ~600 m from the city; `AbandonedVehicle` releases it from the city, and it lies there for 300 s, then goes once no player is within 150 m. A player can take it meanwhile.
- **Anything the city keeps for itself is a fixed prefab in its template, never a roster draw.** The two crab outriders were `prefab: null, role: Rider`, drawn from the roster; once the roster's Rider role became the monowheels they would silently have turned into two more monowheels. `WireStriderCity` names `StriderCrabOutriderBuilder.PrefabPath` (and the scouts' prefabs) outright; only crew are drawn. `StriderCityTemplateTests` asserts nothing else is `prefab: null`.
- **A killed double lets its gunners down, and gives them up.** `MonowheelWreck` → `MountedGunners.ReleaseGunners` unseats them through `VesselSeats.Unseat` (CrewShift's gangway path). They must also leave `MountedGunners.Gunners`: that list is what the mount despawns in `OnNetworkDespawn`, so a gunner left in it would vanish mid-fight when the wreck is taken away. On foot they are party fighters and fold with the party. The step-off point is put on the ground (`PhysicsGroundProbe.TryGroundBelow`, looking down from `stepOffDistance` above it) before `Unseat`, which uses the point as it is when no NavMesh is in reach. A death raised by a restore (`HealthComponent.IsRestoring`, in practice a client receiving the server's death) releases nobody: the riders are the server's to let down, and their reparenting replicates. The living NPC driver is let down too (`NpcPassenger.Dismount`): left seated it vanished with the corpse, was never counted dead, and behaved unlike the gunners.
- **A player-taken wheel must leave its group, or the group takes it back.** While it stayed in `group.Live` it dragged the group centroid after the player; ~350 m on (`despawnRadius`) the party folded and `NpcSpawn.Remove`d the wheel from under its driver; it kept a beaten party "alive" in `IsWipedOut`; and, `NpcSpawn`-disowned, it was not in the save. `NpcWorldSim.ReleaseToPlayer` fixes all four. The prefab still spawns its riders on every instantiate, which is why the stand-down has its own saver (`CrewSaveable`). An NPC never re-takes a wheel a player abandoned. **A defeated wheel must leave its group too, and must not die as a corpse.** Kept in `Live`, a driverless wheel was `NpcSpawn.Remove`d by the party's fold or disband (`DespawnMembers`) the moment players rode ~350 m off, and a wreck went after `HealthReactionModule.despawnDelay` (8 s, `SetActive(false)` on every machine; the field is `corpseLifetime` since 2026-10-05): players lost the wheel they had just won. Strider wheels now carry `corpseLifetime` 0 and `AbandonedVehicle`; a Strider wheel outside any group therefore lies wrecked for good — today only a wheel a player claimed. Releasing at defeat also stops parked and wrecked wheels holding up `IsWipedOut` (an inactive wreck in `Live` used to count as a member).
- **A sweep must stay on streamed ground.** The NavMesh is global, the terrain streams in 500 m chunks 3×3 round the players, and the 600 m ring reaches up to ~950 m from a player: a wheel sent there drives onto unloaded ground and falls. `ScoutRota` pulls waypoints in (or skips them) until `WorldStreamer.IsChunkLoadedAt`; it never pins chunks. Tests inject the check (`ScoutRota.SetGroundCheck`).
- **Steer the effective leader, not the flagged one.** A convoy's lead wheel with no driver is parked and out of `LeaderOf`; steering its `isLeader` flag (as `SteerSpawned` once did) stopped the whole party for good after one hit on the lead driver. The goal read back on a fold follows the effective leader too; the task index still comes from the flagged leader, the only member given the task list.
- **A Strider wheel rides every goal at cruise, never flat out; the cruise speed lives on its `GoalTravelModule` alone.** `NpcWorldSim.SetGoal` / `SteerSpawned` give a convoy leader's `AgentGoal` no speed multiplier, so with the module at 1 a single led at 20 m/s and a double (16) behind it could not hold its slot (the `LeggedDriver` rule above, for wheels). The builder sets `GoalTravelModule.speedMultiplier` = `CruiseSpeed` ÷ top speed (0.7 single, 0.875 double), so a leader and a sweeping scout both ride at 14 m/s while followers, driven by `FormationModule` (not by a goal), still reach their top speed to catch up. The rota's goals therefore carry no multiplier of their own — a second one would multiply to ~10 m/s. `strider-war-party.travelSpeed` (14) is the same speed for the folded party. `StriderMonowheelPrefabTests.Goals_AreRiddenAtCruiseSpeed` pins the wheels, `StriderWarPartyTemplateTests.Folded_ItTravelsAtTheWheelsCruiseSpeed` the template.
- **`regroupDistance` is measured from the leader, not from the slot.** A follower farther than it from the lead house counts as separated and rides for the leader, even standing on its own slot, then turns back for the slot and repeats. The city's scout rows run ~210 m back; at the wheels' first 150 m the rear four oscillated, and at the crab's default 40 m the crabs hovered among the houses. Every city member now takes `RosterAuthoring.CityRegroupDistance` (`CityFarthestSlot` + 30 m, derived, so it follows the template's shape and counts): with the column shuffled, the houses' 120 m and the crawlers' and crabs' 150 m left a crab drawn into slot 12 (≈252 m back) riding for the leader. `StriderCityTemplateTests.EveryFollower_ReachesItsSlot_WithinItsRegroupDistance` checks every non-crew slot against its prefab. `ScoutRota` counts a returning scout home within the same distance, which still lies inside the column's reach.
- **Houses and crawlers have no `HealthComponent`, so they are not fighters** (`GroupMembership.Stamp`) — `CountStanding` counts a member with no health as standing, so a machine enlisted as a fighter could never fall.
- **`FormationModule.SetFormation("")` turns the id into `"caravan"`** — builders write `formationId = ""` through `SerializedFields.SetString`; `NpcWorldSim.Configure` re-keys every member to the group id at spawn.
- **Wholesale builders silently drop hand-set values.** Rebuilding the crawler lost `NetworkObject`, `DontDestroyWithOwner`, the tuned `ClientNetworkTransform`, `SaveableEntity`, `SceneTracked` (Migrate), `UnderTerrainGuard`, `StatusReceiver` and mass 100; `DesertCrawlerBuilder` now writes each itself, and adds `NetworkObject`/`SaveableEntity` before the save so `Chunk_7_4`'s overrides keep their fileIDs. `CrabWalkerBuilder` still cannot reproduce `CrabWalker6` (hand-added guard and mass) — never rebuild that prefab.
- **A root built in an open scene loses `SceneMigrationSynchronization`** (`NetworkObject.OnValidate`), and a later probe-and-apply bakes it off again. Every Strider builder calls `NetworkObjectDefaults.KeepSceneMigrationSync(path)` on the saved asset as its very last write — after its own save and after the `SaveableWiring` / `RagdollWiring` passes ([Multiplayer.md](Multiplayer.md) Gotchas); `StriderNetworkPrefabTests` guards it. Those passes edit through `LoadPrefabContents` (a preview scene, `buildIndex` -1), which `CheckForInScenePlaced` ignores, so today they never switch it off; calling it last keeps that true if one ever edits in the open scene.
- **The leader's `travelTimeout` must outlast the walk to its furthest stop.** `NpcTaskModule`'s default 240 s is ~650 m at the city's 2.7 m/s, but the search picks sites up to 2500 m away: the leader gave up and re-chose every four minutes and almost never arrived. `StriderCityBuilder.CityTravelTimeout` = `CitySearchRadius / CityLeaderSpeed × 1.5` (~1389 s); `StriderHabitatWalkerTests.TheLeader_OutlastsTheWalkToItsFurthestStop` pins it. Raise the search radius and the timeout follows; lower the leader's speed and it follows too.
- **A registered site's own `Radius` decides arrival, not the task's `arriveRadius` 25** (`NpcTaskPlanner.ResolveDestination`); the 25 m applies only to roam points. No Ruin or ScrapField marker exists today, so the city always roams. Once one is placed, a `WorldSiteMarker` at its default 12 m radius may be unreachable for a 21 m hull whose pivot is held off by the ruin's geometry — the leader then walks at it for the whole `CityTravelTimeout` before re-choosing. Give Ruin/ScrapField markers a radius ≥ 25 m; taking `Max(site.Radius, task.arriveRadius)` in the planner would also fix it, but changes arrival for every other tasked NPC whose task radius exceeds its site's (authored task radii 10–30 m), so it was left alone.
- **Crew posts come from `COL_Deck` and `COL_Hull`.** Posts from the deck alone put 4 of 6 inside the house; posts now stand midway across the open ring. The side strip is ~1.1 m wide ([Vehicles.md](Vehicles.md) Gotchas).
- **Builders dirty `Bootstrap.unity`** (the habitat builder instantiates the RigWalker into the active scene). Reopen it from disk before a headless test run, which otherwise writes `ABORTED=dirty-scene` ([Testing.md](Testing.md)).
- **At runtime the city's ground is measured on the NavMesh, not the heightmap or colliders.** A stop is up to 2500 m away, and chunk heightmaps and colliders exist only in the 3×3 loaded chunks round the players, so both are absent where the choice is made; the world NavMesh is baked whole and loaded on every machine. The measure is the ship's (`LevelGroundSearch` / `HullFootprint`); only the sampler differs, and the editor's start search uses the heightmaps of the chunks it opens. The NavMesh is the walkable surface: rocks and buildings are holes, so a footprint sample landing in one (nearest mesh > `sampleTolerance` 10 m sideways), or on ground > `sampleReach` 30 m above or below the candidate, rejects the spot. The two sources disagree near features (measured on the same footprints: NavMesh relief 0.1–25 m under the heightmap's), so the start and the stops are not judged identically.
- **The start search is a grid, not a fan of bearings, and takes the nearest level spot.** The spawn is ~210 m from the map's east edge and ~430 m from its north edge, so a 437 m footprint fits only to the west and south. 16 bearings × 17 distances left 175 of 272 footprints off the terrain (`Complete` false) and stepped past the pan 600 m west and the one 570 m south-west; the only level candidates left were the Clanker town's own basin, inside its clearance, so the search failed. A full grid finds them.
- **The desert is hilly at the city's scale, so level ground is scarce.** Measured round the spawn: heightmap relief across the 435 m footprint at 20 start candidates was min 20.4, median 53 and max 195 m; the old start (90° off the away bearing) stood on ~130 m of it. 3° keeps roughly the flattest tenth. From the old start, `TryLevelRoamPoint` found level ground in 33 of 40 choices. Raising `CityMaxSlopeDegrees` or shrinking the footprint trades flatness for choice.
- **A folded city can still stop on a slope.** `NpcWorldSim.ChooseTask` falls back to `RoamPointUnsampled` whenever `ResolveDestination` returns false, which the level rule now does when 8 candidates find nothing (~1 choice in 5 from the old start). Such a stop is replaced as soon as the city unfolds (a spawned leader re-chooses in `Choosing`), but a player who arrives while the folded city dwells there meets its crew ashore on that ground. The fix is in `NpcWorldSim.ChooseTask`: skip the unsampled fallback for a task whose `levelGround.Enabled`, and retry on the next tick instead.
- **A level choice costs the server up to ~40 ms.** Each of the 8 attempts is one `TryRoamPoint` (3.4 ms, for its 625 m NavMesh sample) plus up to 153 footprint samples (0.009 ms each at a 30 m reach, 0.04 ms at 60 m). It runs once per stop, every few minutes, but raising `attempts`, `searchRadius` or `sampleReach` shows up as a frame hitch.
- **Clearing the Clanker town means clearing it with the whole column.** The old 300 m clearance was measured from the leader, but the city's slots reach ~217 m behind it and the town's alarm ring is 170 m. The only level ground near the spawn is the basin the town itself was sited on, 304–346 m from it, so followers would have stood inside the ring. `ClankerTownClearance` = `ClankerSettlementBuilder.AlarmRadius` + the footprint radius.
- **A barge is longer than a row is deep.** A barge is 34 m long with its pivot 22 m from its stern (15 m wide). At the walking spike's 30 m rows anything in the next row of its lane (offset only 10 m sideways by the odd-row stagger) overlapped it, and so would a second barge. Rows are now 35 m (a scout behind a barge, every offset at its worst), and a barge's card (`keepsClear`) holds it clear of every member, measured from the prefabs' renderers by `StriderCityColumn.Card` (particle systems left out — the dust's unsimulated bounds are not the hull), with jitter and drift at their worst or both parked `slotTolerance` off their slots. The deal only lays a card where it passes, and `StriderCityTemplateTests.EverySeed_DealsAColumnThatKeepsTheRules_AndTheWorldsDiffer` deals the written template for 300 seeds. The houses' and crawlers' own spacing is the spike's and is not judged (their renderer bounds include the leg sweep). **The column is dealt, never rejection-sampled, and its cards are measured data:** Shuffling the whole deck and keeping the first order that passes accepted 4 shuffles in 20 000 for this city (87 % break the carrier rule alone) — ~35 ms of rule checks per spawn and some seeds with none in 20 000. `ColumnDeal` lays one card at a time with an incremental check, prunes when the carriers left cannot still reach their first slots, and restarts a deal past `LaidPerDeal` (400) cards: ~6 ms per spawn on average in the editor, 36 ms at worst over 500 seeds. A card's footprint is copied from its prefab when `Wire Strider City` runs; rebuild a vehicle bigger and the deal still uses the old size until it is re-run (`StriderCityTemplateTests.EveryFollower_IsACardOfTheDeck_WithItsRulesAndFootprint` fails). A kindless card's `kind` is `""`, as the scene serializes a null.
- **Longer rows push the rear scouts out of reach.** The 35 m rows moved `CityFarthestSlot` from ≈217 m to ≈322 m. The members' `regroupDistance` is derived from it (`CityRegroupDistance`, 352 m); without a rebuild the rear members ride for the leader from their own slots. The level-ground footprint did **not** grow: it is the farthest slot a **carrier** may take (`CityFarthestCarrierSlot` over `CityCarrierSlots`, ≈218.5 m), the ground a crew steps off onto, so stops are as easy to find as before. The Clanker clearance takes the whole column.
- **Add dust after anything that measures renderer bounds.** A particle system is a `Renderer`: with the cloud added before `StriderCrabOutriderBuilder.AttachRider` sized the shell, the rider's seat went up 11 m ([VehicleDust.md](VehicleDust.md)).
- **Not yet verified (Task 15):** the barges holding formation over dunes (no sinking or floating), their crews stepping off and boarding, a player walking a moving barge's deck (an interpolated kinematic body written directly may render stepped), a client's view of the barges, save/reload mid-stop with barge crews ashore; a scout pair's sweep and return in play (host and client), host + client play, save/reload mid-march and mid-stop, profiling beside the city (now 20 machines + 30 crew), the gangway's clearance from the leg swing, the crab's faster footfall, the deck posts' clearance from the rails.

## Extending

- **More or fewer crew:** add or remove an entry in `StriderCityBuilder.PostLayout` (a side of the hull and a `PostRow`: front corner, mid-side or back corner; a new spot on the deck needs a new `PostRow`), rebuild the habitat, re-run `Wire Strider City`. `CrewPosts` is `PostLayout.Length`, and `WireStriderCity`'s crew counts follow it (half Warriors, the rest Scouts). The houses/workers/scouts/outriders counts are named constants in `RosterAuthoring`.
- **Another tribe whose home moves:** a carrier prefab with `VesselSeats` + `CrewShift` + a gangway marker, `crew: true` specs listed after the carriers, a house flagged leader with an `NpcTaskModule`, and `holdSlotAtRest` on anything too big to share a rest ring — follow [spacegame-tribe](.claude/skills/spacegame-tribe/SKILL.md) for the tribe itself.
- **More barges, or another kind:** add a `(base, variant)` row to `StriderBargeBuilder.Barges` and the variant's name to `EntityFactionWiring.Assignments`, rebuild, re-run `Wire Strider City`, and it checks the deck can still be dealt (`ColumnDeal.TryDeal` logs an error otherwise: raise `CityCarrierRows` or widen `CityShape`); `StriderCityTemplateTests` deals it for 300 seeds.
- **Changing `CityShape` or the city's member counts** (`RosterAuthoring`) moves the farthest city slot, and every city member's `FormationModule.regroupDistance` is derived from it (`CityRegroupDistance`): re-run **Build Strider Monowheels**, **Build Strider Barges**, the habitat, crawler and crab builders after `Wire Strider City`, or scouts in the outer slots oscillate between slot and leader (`StriderCityTemplateTests` checks slot + jitter < `regroupDistance`).
- **Flatter or rougher stops:** change `StriderCityBuilder.CityMaxSlopeDegrees` (or the other `CityLevel*` consts), then re-run **Wire Strider City**, which rewrites every stop's `levelGround` and re-picks the start (`StriderCityTemplateTests` checks that every task carries `CityLevelGround`). The footprint follows `CityFarthestSlot`, so a bigger column needs flatter land: expect `TryLevelRoamPoint` to fail more often and the start search to log its error.
- **A killable house** is its own feature: a `HealthComponent` makes it a fighter and a ragdoll/save candidate; decide what happens to its seated crew first.
