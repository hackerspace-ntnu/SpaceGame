# Hand tools

Props a Raxy carries: hammers, shovels, spears, buckets, baskets, tanks, carts, an electric harpoon gun. They are ordinary inventory items with no effect of their own, so they reach the world, the hotbar and a save exactly as [Inventory.md](Inventory.md) describes. They are **not** listed in the artifact browser (O): `InventoryItem.showInDevBrowser` is off for every one.

## Model

- **Source:** rows `Deco_Tools*` and `Deco_Carriers` of `_Source~/models/buildings/decorations.blend` (89 `Coll_Deco_Tool_*` / `Coll_Deco_Carry_*`). The `Root_*` empty **is the grip point**; Blender +Z (the business end) is Unity +Y, Blender −Y (the working face or muzzle) is Unity +Z — measured against the built prefabs, it holds. Exported one FBX each to `Art/Models/Items/Tools/<Category>/<id lowercase>.fbx`.
- **Item:** [CarriedToolItem](Assets/Game/Scripts/Items/Tools/CarriedToolItem.cs) (a `ToolItem` whose `Use()` does nothing) + `ItemGrip` + optional [BeltMount](Assets/Game/Scripts/Items/Tools/BeltMount.cs). `useAction` plays a body action on use; only **one-shot** actions are wired (Stab, Spear Throw, Sword Strike, Lasso Throw, Plant Seedling, Poke Ground) because a Loop action such as Hammer would start and never end.
- **Stance, not numbers.** A roster row names a [`CarryStance`](Assets/Game/Editor/Items/HandTools/CarryStance.cs) and nothing else about how it is held; [`GripFitter`](Assets/Game/Editor/Items/HandTools/GripFitter.cs) turns that into the pose and the `ItemGrip` offsets. A stance is a pose plus two reference axes (the tool's along-axis and working face) and where each should point on the holder; the fitter holds the real pose on a real Raxy, reads the hand frame, and solves the rotation. Nobody types a rotation.

  | Stance | Pose | One or two hands | Meaning |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `Wield` | `Ready` | one | short tool, head up-forward at 50° |
  | `Staff` | `Carry` | one | long haft at the side, leaning 30° forward |
  | `Hang` | `Carry` | one | vessel by its handle, rim up |
  | `Aim` | `OneHanded` | per override | gun, spyglass, shield face: item +Z forward |
  | `Push` | `Push` | **both** | the two `Carry_Cart_*` items only: cart ahead of the holder. The clip raises a Raxy's hands above its head (measured 2.6 m, head 2.42 m), so nothing pushes a real cart with it: see [Pushables.md](Pushables.md) |

- **Roster:** [HandToolRoster](Assets/Game/Editor/Items/HandTools/HandToolRoster.cs) is the one table: stance, hold size, optional `PoseOverride`, `ItemAlong`/`ItemFace` (a scraper's handle lies along +X), `Nudge` (metres, holder space), `CarryOnly` (the two carts). [HandToolBuilder](Assets/Game/Editor/Items/HandTools/HandToolBuilder.cs) rebuilds prefab, item, icon and network entry from it; GUIDs survive a re-run. **How a tool stows is not typed per row**: [BeltHangs](Assets/Game/Editor/Items/HandTools/BeltHangs.cs) derives it from the stance and the model's own bounds (below). A row only says something of its own where the rule is wrong: `HangPoint` + `HangDown` (31 rows, a loop authored from the Blender manifests), `HangAlong` (the shield stands on its edge), `HangOut` (the sickle's blade must lie along the leg), `Slung` (the sifting pan is too broad for a hip).
- **Poses:** [HoldClipGenerator](Assets/Game/Editor/Animation/HoldClipGenerator.cs) writes the one-armed hold clips and points the humanoid profile at them. `Carry` is the library's `Idle_Loop` (arms down), `Ready` is `Sword_Idle`'s right arm with the left arm taken from the idle (`Hold_Ready.anim`), `Push` is `Push_Loop`. **Every older hold style is a gun clip that keys both arms**, which is why a bucket-carrier used to raise its free hand like a pistol grip; these three leave the off arm hanging. **`Carry` and `Ready` are arms-only** (`HoldPose.armsOnly`): they play on the `Hold Arms` layer, whose mask has no body part, so a holder walks with the walk's own spine (hips to head 88 degrees, as empty-handed; 72 and 46 before). `Push` leans into the cart on the Upper Body layer on purpose. See [HumanoidAnimation.md](HumanoidAnimation.md) Gotchas.
- **Belt and pack:** the hang points are **not** on the Raxy. Each belt and backpack garment prefab carries a [GarmentMounts](Assets/Game/Scripts/Items/Tools/GarmentMounts.cs): a bone (`Hips` for belts, `Spine` for the pack) and one child transform per `BeltSlot` (`HipRight`, `HipLeft`, `Back`, `PackLeft`, `PackRight`). A point is authored in the garment's **mesh space** (what its `RestPose` view shows in Prefab Mode): origin at the loop or ring, +Y up toward the garment, +Z out from the body. [BeltSeat.CreateAnchors](Assets/Game/Scripts/Items/Tools/BeltSeat.cs) carries each point through the garment's bind pose to a bone-parented anchor, so one set of points fits every Raxy whatever its model scale. `BeltSeat.Hang` lays an item's `BeltHang` child on an anchor. Points placed: `Belt.standard` / `Belt.poncho` hips + back, `Work_belt` left hoop + back, `skirt_workBelt` right hoop + left + back, `Backpack` and `Backpack_Large` both low back corners; `Work_Belt_Flask` offers none (the flask is the right hip). Added 2026-10-04 from the generator's own belt geometry: `Duelist_Gunbelt` (both rear hips, the holsters own the sides, + back), `Mechanic_ToolHarness`, `Lineman_Harness`, `Bounty_Trophies` and `Miner_Gear` (hips + back), `Engineer_ValveKit` (hips only: the wrench rides the back), `Sapper_Detonator` (rear right hip + back: the plunger and spools own the sides); pack corners on `Gunsmith_PartsCase` and `Farmer_Basket`.
- **Where a tool hangs** is the tool's own [BeltMount](Assets/Game/Scripts/Items/Tools/BeltMount.cs): the `BeltHang` child (the point, which way is up, which way is out) and `slots`, the slots it may use, best first. [BeltSeat.Plan](Assets/Game/Scripts/Items/Tools/BeltSeat.cs) is the one pure planner (first listed slot the wearer offers and no earlier bag item took); [BeltCarrier](Assets/Game/Scripts/agents/Entity/BeltCarrier.cs) hangs the bag by it. **Three classes, from the stance and the size** (`BeltHangs`, the model's bounds at the size it was built, `HipMaxLength` 0.9 m):

  | Class | Who | Hangs | Slots |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Short tool | `Wield` / `Aim` up to 0.9 m (34 tools) | by the end of its handle (12% in from the butt), business end down, its thinnest side out so it lies flat to the thigh | `HipRight, HipLeft, PackLeft, PackRight, Back` |
  | Vessel | `Hang` stance up to 0.9 m (19 tools: buckets, baskets, tanks, pots, flasks) | by its handle exactly as from a fist, rim up, held out by whatever is wider than 0.1 m of thigh | same as short |
  | Long tool | over 0.9 m, 34 tools: all `Staff` (spears, pickaxe, shovel, hoe, broom, rod, bow, flag, net), the longer clubs/crowbar/ladle/mallet/brush, the harpoon gun, the shield, yoke, throwing net, watering can, sifting pan | slung across the back, held 25% up from the butt, leaning 15 degrees so the head rides over one shoulder, flat to the back (thinnest side out) | `Back, PackLeft, PackRight` only: **never a hip** |

  **Carry-only, the whole list:** `Carry_Cart_Hand` and `Carry_Cart_Hover` (`CarryOnly`): a wheeled cart of 3.0 to 3.5 m cannot hang from a person, and carts are real pushables now ([Pushables.md](Pushables.md)): the Drover pushes one and holds no item, so no resident holds either of these. They remain only as inventory items, as `ResidentTuning.carryItems` index entries (append only) and as the item of the pen's `Carry_Cart_Hand__01` prop that haulers carry; retiring them is listed in Pushables.md. 87 of the 89 tools hang somewhere (`HandToolBeltTests`).
- **Residents:** the archetype says what a profession carries (`ResidentArchetype.heldItem`, `beltItems`); [ResidentCarry](Assets/Game/Scripts/agents/Residents/Core/ResidentCarry.cs) fills an empty bag from it before the hand draws slot 0. [RaxyToolLoadouts](Assets/Game/Editor/Items/HandTools/RaxyToolLoadouts.cs) holds the profession table (17 of 20; storyteller, elder, villager carry nothing) and adds the bag, hand, belt, `ResidentCarry` and savers to every Raxy prefab that has a `Resident` (**Equip Residents**). **Every Raxy resident wears a belt:** `EnsureBelt` dresses one that has no worn `GarmentMounts` on the hips with `Clothes_Belt.standard` (`Clothes_Belt.poncho` if it wears a `Clothes_Poncho*`), as a garment prefab instance parented to the prefab **root**, the way the hand-dressed `Raxy_*` prefabs wear theirs. A Raxy that already wears any hip belt (`Work_belt`, `skirt_workBelt`, either `Belt.*`) keeps it. `Drifter_RaxyClassic` has no `Jaw`: until 2026-10-04 no garment could bind to it and it stayed beltless. `SkinnedGarment.Bind` now lets a skeleton lack a bone the garment's mesh gives **no weight** (`WeightedBones`; the missing slot is bound to the wearer's root, which a zero-weight bone never moves), so a belt, a pack and any garment skinned to the 66 other bones bind to the Classic. A garment weighted to the jaw (a mask, a respirator) still does not. **Dressed 2026-10-04:** `RaxyToolLoadouts.WirePrefab` was run on the four resident prefabs that wore no belt (`Drifter_RaxyClassic`, `Raxy_Job_Farmer`, `Raxy_Type_SandRacer`, `Raxy_Type_Wastelander`; not the whole menu, which re-saves every resident prefab including the ones other people have open) and the Drover archetype was assigned from the table (`Tool_Lasso` in the hand, no belt item). The Classic wears `Clothes_Belt.standard` with all 67 bones bound (the unused `Jaw` slot rides the root) and hangs a hammer, chisel and whetstone on its right hip, left hip and back (edit-mode render, front, both sides and back).

## Flows

Resident hand: `ResidentHands` decides what is in the hand ([Residents.md](Residents.md)): the tool for work, nothing otherwise. Putting the tool away is just emptying the hand (`Unequip`); `BeltCarrier` hangs it on its own anchor. `BeltCarrier.CanStow(slot)` says whether the item has a `BeltMount` and an anchor of its own; if not the resident keeps holding it.

Draw and stow are instant as state and travel as a picture: `BeltCarrier` starts the new instance (the hook's after a stow, the hand's after a draw) where the old one was and carries it across over `BeltCarrier.TransitSeconds` (`ResidentTuning.toolTransitSeconds`, 0.25 s, set by `ResidentPresence`; 0 for any other wearer) with [`ToolTransit`](Assets/Game/Scripts/agents/Entity/ToolTransit.cs), each end held in its bone's space so it follows the body. `HoldAnimator` clears or sets the hold style in the same call as before, so the gesture gate sees an empty hand at once and a gesture may start while the tool is still on its way to the hook (the tool is already out of the hand); a drawn tool arrives over the hold pose's own 0.15 s blend. Nothing is sent: every machine derives the hand from the replicated activity and plays the same move. **Seen 2026-10-04 in edit-mode renders** (the real `BeltCarrier`, inventory and equipment of a `Drifter_Raxy 2` driven through Awake and `LateUpdate` by hand, the animator stepped 0.05 s at a time, six frames each way): the hammer swings out of the Ready grip, over the thigh and onto the hook head down over 0.25 s, and back; its position moves monotonically between the two and lands exactly where the socket seated it. Not seen in Play.

Equip: `EntityEquipmentController` → `EquipItemSocket` seats the grip point in the palm at `handFrame * Euler(rotationOffset)`; `HoldAnimator` writes the item's `Style` into the Upper Body layer. Belt: `BeltCarrier.LateUpdate` notices a changed hand slot or bag and re-hangs.

Build: **Tools ▸ SpaceGame ▸ Animation ▸ Generate Hold Clips** (once, or after changing poses), then **Items ▸ Hand Tools ▸ Build All**. Build All fits every tool, then seats each built prefab through the real `EquipItemSocket` and logs `[HandTools] X: seated N degrees off its Y stance` if the tool's along-axis is more than 1° from the stance, and `turned N degrees about its length` if its working face is (the rotation the along axis cannot see).

Look at it: **Items ▸ Hand Tools ▸ Preview Held Contact Sheets** → `Temp/HandToolPreview/held_<page>.png`, ten tools a page in roster order (the log names them), each as the whole body from the side, then the hand from the front and from the side framed on the palm; `HandToolPreview.PreviewHeld(ids, tile)` with a larger `tile` (560) is the close look at a few. **Items ▸ Hand Tools ▸ Audit Grips** and **Audit Work Clips** write `Temp/HandToolPreview/grip_audit.csv` and `work_audit.csv` (the tables in [Grip audit](hand-tool-grip-audit.md)). **Items ▸ Hand Tools ▸ Preview Held And Belt** → `Temp/HandToolPreview/<Id>.png`, one row per tool: whole body, hand close, hand side, belt close, belt back, whole body from the side. **Items ▸ Hand Tools ▸ Preview Stowed Contact Sheets** → `Temp/HandToolPreview/stowed_<page>.png`, 12 tools a page in roster order (each seen from the side its anchor faces, then from the front or the wearer's right; the log names the tools), the quick way to check all 89 after a change to `BeltHangs`. Headless: `-executeMethod SpaceGame.EditorTools.HandToolPreview.PreviewFromCommandLine -handTools Id,Id`.

## Multiplayer

Every machine builds the same bag from the instance's archetype and draws the same belt; nothing is sent. A resident's drawn/stowed state comes from the replicated activity and prop on every machine (`ResidentHands`). A bag changed at runtime on the server (a looted tool) is **not** replicated to clients, as `EntityInventoryComponent` is local state. Item prefabs are registered network prefabs like all items. **Not yet seen on a second machine** (the `settlement-*` autotest now logs `hand=` and `hold=` per resident on both machines to compare) — the pose and grip are baked data, so there is nothing host-only about them, but it is unverified.

## Persistence

Nothing of its own: the bag is `EntityInventorySaveable`, the hand slot `EntityEquipmentSaveable`, the belt is derived. A resident's hand slot is re-derived from its activity every frame (`ResidentHands`), so a restored slot never outlives the next state. A dropped tool persists as any dropped item. `showInDevBrowser` is an asset field, not state. A band's kit weapon lent to a resident at home is **never saved**: `ExpeditionMember` reports the slot through `ILentSlots` and `EntityInventorySaveable` writes it empty, keeping every other slot's position.

## Gotchas

- **The Raxy's grip frame comes from its fingers, and its root is moved to the middle of the closed fist (2026-10-03).** The Raxy has three fingers and a thumb. [`HandGripFrame`](Assets/Game/Scripts/Items/Equipped/HandGripFrame.cs) wanted index, little finger and thumb, found no little finger, and fell to the forearm path: a roll guessed from the world's up at the moment of the call, an origin 5 cm from the wrist (a fraction of a length that was 0.4 of the forearm) and the fist about 19 cm out. Every tool lay along the wrist, behind the fist; the hammer's handle passed through the wrist cuff. The frame now takes the ring finger as the far side of the knuckle row (`pinky == null` falls to ring), which is anatomical and the same on every Raxy prefab whatever pose it is in when it spawns. Its origin is still a fraction of the hand (9 cm from the wrist) and the closed fingers' loop is about 19 cm out, so [`GripFitter`](Assets/Game/Editor/Items/HandTools/GripFitter.cs) adds [`HandToolRig.FistCentre`](Assets/Game/Editor/Items/HandTools/HandToolRig.cs) (the mean of the index, middle and ring joints in the pose the tool is held in) to every `positionOffset`: the canonical hand socket for tools. The astronaut's hand is 1.7 times a human's and its frame, which has a little finger, is unchanged; a tool picked up by the player seats its root at the astronaut's palm plus the same metres (about 10 cm along the fingers), not at its fist centre, and nobody has looked at that. `HandToolGripTests.TheRaxyHandFrame_IsDerivedFromItsFingers` fails if the Raxy falls back to the forearm path again.
- **The rig puts back the hips as well as the head, legs and root (2026-10-03).** The hold clips are full-body and the Upper Body mask has no Root, so the game never applies their hips; the rig kept them, and a gun clip's hips lean the whole torso back. Every non-`Carry` fit was therefore made against a leaning body: measured in the faithful body the old `Wield` fit pointed 10 degrees off its stance, the old `Aim` fit 6 and the old `Push` fit 46 (the carts). `Carry` was unaffected (its hips are the idle's). An arms-only pose also gets its spine, chest and upper chest put back (`restTorso`), because on the `Hold Arms` layer the walk owns them.
- **A render after the first in one editor command shows a stale skin.** A skinned mesh is skinned once per frame, so a preview that poses the rig between renders shows the arm hanging while the tool, parented to the bone, is where the bone really is: it looks as if every tool floats beside the fist. [`HandToolRig.Bake`](Assets/Game/Editor/Items/HandTools/HandToolRig.cs) hides each skinned renderer and draws a baked copy for as long as the scope lives; the held previews use it. The older belt previews still do not, which is harmless only while the pose does not change between their renders.
- **The fitter owes its truth to [HandToolRig](Assets/Game/Editor/Items/HandTools/HandToolRig.cs).** Sampling a full-body hold clip also carries the root and the legs, which the Upper Body layer never applies, and a library clip need not face the way the Raxy does. The rig therefore samples a rest pose first (`Carry`'s idle), turns the body to face +Z from its hip line, and puts the head, legs and root back after every pose sample. Without that, every direction a stance names is relative to a body facing the wrong way: the cart came out behind the Raxy and the hammer pointed at its back, with the numbers looking plausible.
- **Offsets are baked from a pose.** Change a hold clip, a stance, or the Raxy rig and the stored `rotationOffset` is stale until **Build All** runs again. `HandToolGripTests` checks the built pose matches the stance; the residual check in the build checks the aim.
- **Only `Push` and the harpoon gun use two hands.** `HandToolGripTests` fails if another tool does. Growing the list is a decision.
- **The cart item is scaled up on purpose** (the real cart is [Pushables.md](Pushables.md)). A Raxy is ~1.5× a human, so a hand cart at its honest 2.4 m puts the wheels in the air at hand height; `Carry_Cart_Hand` holds at 3.5 m and `Carry_Cart_Hover` at 3.0 m with a 0.18 m `Nudge` so the shafts meet the hands. `packSize` stays 0.9.
- The electric harpoon gun is a model and an inert item; it fires nothing. No tool is `menacing` — nothing here fires a shot, which `MenaceSensor` requires.
- Tools longer than ~1.4 m carry `packSize` 0.9 so they can be stowed; `PackSizeTests` skips the `Items/Tools` folder for that reason.
- `export_collections` makes parts flat siblings; sheaths, wire and loaded harpoon are separate renderers, not hidden automatically.
- **A garment's own transform is meaningless once worn.** A skinned garment's root sits at an arbitrary FBX offset that differs per Raxy prefab, so mount points are read through the bind pose, never through the root's world position. `BakeMesh` plus `TransformPoint` over-scales garments with a non-1 root scale; measure by skinning the dominant bone by hand.
- **`Carry` and `Ready` no longer bend the walk** (hips→head 88° empty-handed and with either, was 72° and 46°; the aimed styles still do: 75, 71, 46, `Push` 79; [HumanoidAnimation.md](HumanoidAnimation.md) Gotchas). Residents still stow the tool for travel, but a carried bucket, a tool with no belt anchor and a worker who moves no longer stoop.
- **`Lantern` and `SignalFlag` used to override their pose to `Torch`,** a gun-style clip that keys both arms, which raised the free hand. They hold in `Carry` now.
- **A tool's grip is fitted to an idle pose and a work clip puts the hand somewhere else.** The grip is the same relative to the hand in both, so the audit's work table is what to read: the shaft angle is how far the tool lies from the line between the two hands in a two-handed clip, the lowest tip how near its business end gets to the soles. Raising the tip to the ground is not a grip problem (the hoe's tip never gets below 1.2 m in `Farm Plough` at any rotation: the tool is longer than the prop the clip was captured with); that is the station and reach work of phase 4 of [the activity-contract plan](../../superpowers/plans/2026-10-03-activity-contract-animation.md).
- **A resident draws its tool to work and stows it otherwise** (`ResidentHands`, `ResidentHandsRule`; equip and unequip are instant and `HoldAnimator` clears `HoldStyle` in the same call, so `BodyHands.Busy` reads None a frame later). `BeltCarrier.CanStow` is false for an item with no `BeltMount` (only the two carts) and for one none of whose slots the wearer offers or that earlier items took; taking it out of the hand would leave it hanging nowhere, so that tool stays in the hand, the holder never gestures, and it is logged once per item (`[Residents] X stays in the hand of an idle resident`).
- **A hidden body changes nothing in the hand** (`ResidentPresence.Update` skips it): a tool put on the belt of a body whose renderers were switched off at hide time would hang visibly in the air. It stows when the resident leaves the door.
- **A conversation's first line can land before the tool is away**: `Talking` is published by the 0.5 s routine pass, a line is said the frame the focus starts. The opening `greet` is skipped (hands busy); the talk loop and beats follow. Not seen in a real conversation yet.
- **A station says which tool it wants (2026-10-03, [Stations.md](Stations.md)).** `ResidentHands.Station(heldCue)` draws a tool from `CharacterCue.Tools` when the resident carries one (its usual tool if that is among them, else the first in the bag, so a cook at the chopping board draws its belt cleaver) and puts the tool away for a `BareHands` cue (wiping, rummaging, tending) when it can be stowed. The Brewer and Apprentice archetype assets were changed to match their posts (ladle, hammer); `RaxyToolLoadouts` lists the same rows (Brewer: ladle, flask, water tank; Apprentice: hammer, chisel, trowel; Drover: lasso), checked against the assets 2026-10-04, so *Equip Residents* no longer undoes them.
- Seen live (host, 70 residents): stowable tools on the belt while walking or sitting, in the hand at work, a gesture pick empty with the tool in hand and real once it is away, `Talking` stows and `Work` draws within two frames. No client, no reload run (re-deriving a restored slot is by design, not yet seen).
- **`BeltCarrier` plans anchors over the WHOLE bag in slot order, hand included** (`BeltSeat.Plan`): an item's anchor does not depend on what is drawn, so drawing leaves a gap on the belt and nothing reshuffles. An item with no anchor left is simply not drawn and stays in the bag unseen (the first slots win, and `ResidentCarry` puts the held tool in slot 0, so **the tool a resident works with always gets its anchor before any belt item**). Audited 2026-10-03 over every `NomadCulture` profile prefab x archetype (71 combinations): all held tools can be stowed except on `Drifter_RaxyClassic` (no belt: Farmer's hoe, Miller's mallet, Cook's ladle, Waterkeeper's pump stay in the hand) and the Drover's cart; belt items left without an anchor, so unseen: Cook's flask on `Raxy_clothed` / Classic, Gardener's sickle on `Drifter_rax_young` (both `Work_belt`, which offers only `Back` + `HipLeft`: a long held tool takes the Back and the first belt item the left hoop), Miner's shovel on `Drifter_RaxyAsh` (two long tools, one `Back` on a belt; with a backpack it takes a pack corner), plus Classic's belt items.
- `BeltCarrier` searches the whole character for `GarmentMounts` (garments sit beside the Animator, not under it). A Raxy wearing no belt or pack hangs nothing and logs once per item; the first garment offering a slot wins.
- **A belt is any worn garment whose `GarmentMounts.Bone` is `Hips`, and only an ACTIVE one counts.** `BeltSeat.CreateAnchors` uses `GetComponentsInChildren<GarmentMounts>()`, which skips inactive objects, so a belt switched off in the prefab hangs nothing. `Clothes_Work_Belt_Flask` is a belt to the eye and offers nothing; `Work_belt` offers only `Back` + `HipLeft`, so a hammer that prefers `HipRight` falls to the first free slot in enum order.
- **A belt on a skeleton without a bone the garment USES is invisible and inert.** `Drifter_RaxyClassic` (raxy_classic.fbx) lacks `Jaw` and nothing else of the 67 bones every garment names. Since 2026-10-04 a missing bone the mesh gives no weight no longer stops the bind (see Model), so the belt should fit; a garment weighted to a missing bone still logs "has no bone 'X'" and `EnsureBelt` destroys it instead of saving a dead garment. Re-syncing the Classic rig (adding a `Jaw`) is still the user's art task and would make masks fit too. **Looked at 2026-10-04:** the belt sits snug on the Classic's hips and the three tools hang where the slots say (right hip head down, left hip, back), from the front, both sides and behind.
- **Equip Residents resaves every resident prefab** and so also drops dead serialized fields (the old `memoryDuration` / `spotId` / `anchors` block on every Raxy). Harmless, but it shows up in the diff of prefabs the run did not otherwise change; revert those if the diff should hold only the belts. It also re-serializes archetype assets (new fields such as `chore`, `duty`).
- **Re-running *Build Drifter NPCs* / *Sync Drifter Rigs* can undress a belt.** Belts added here are root children, which `DrifterClothes.Dress` (it only touches children of `Model`) leaves alone; a wholesale rebuild of a Drifter prefab does not. Run **Equip Residents** afterwards.
- **Seen 2026-10-03 (preview Raxy `Raxy_handyman`: skirt work belt + backpack, all five slots):** all 87 hung tools rendered on contact sheets, from the anchor's side and the front. Long tools lie across the back with the head over the left shoulder; hip tools hang head down; vessels hang from their handle at the hip. Not seen: a Raxy in any other garments (a skirt hides a hip tool from the front: the tool is inside the cloth, which is not solid), a walking gait (the tools hang off the Hips bone, so they follow its swing), a long tool through a loose cape or poncho. A tank or a wide basket on a hip still overlaps the thigh by about 0.1 m (`VesselBodyDepth`) rather than float away from the hook.
- **A band's kit is not the resident's bag** ([Expeditions.md](Expeditions.md)). A stand-in's bag IS its kit — weapon, tool, belt items, in that order, so the weapon is slot 0 and gets its anchor first — written on every machine from the replicated kit index before `ResidentCarry.Start` (which fills only an empty bag); a kit longer than the bag (4) is cut with a warning. A resident at home with its band (`IsWithBand`) keeps its own bag and is LENT the kit weapon in a free slot on every machine (nothing if it already carries that item or has no room — its hands then report the missing tool), taken back when it is handed off or home. Either way `Activity.Expedition` needs the tool and `Resident.HeldItem` is the kit weapon; a spear holds in its own `Staff` stance, i.e. `Carry`.
- The held grip and the preview use the right hand; a left-handed holder plays the mirrored state, and the mirrored grip has not been looked at.

## Extending

Model a `Coll_Deco_Tool_<Name>` with its root at the grip, export it, add a roster row naming a `CarryStance` and a hold size, run **Build All** (it stows by the rule above, with no hang row), look at the preview and the stowed contact sheet, put it in a profession row in `RaxyToolLoadouts` and run **Equip Residents**. A tool that hangs wrongly gets `HangAlong` / `HangOut` / `Slung` or an authored `HangPoint`, not a new class. A tool whose root is not where a hand would take it gets `GripShift` (metres along the tool, positive toward the business end; `AGripShift_SlidesTheToolAlongItsOwnLength`), and one whose handle is not along +Y gets `ItemAlong`/`ItemFace`. After any change run **Audit Grips**: its flags are in the appendix. A new way of holding something is a new `CarryStance` row in `CarryStances.Of` plus, if no existing pose fits, a hold style and clip in `HoldClipGenerator`.
