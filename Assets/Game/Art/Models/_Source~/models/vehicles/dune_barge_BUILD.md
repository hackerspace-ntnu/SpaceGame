# Dune barge — build record

`dune_barge.blend`, generated by `dune_barge.py` (with `dune_barge_hull.py` for the hull and
`dune_barge_tracks.py` for the track maths), from the user's concept image of a rusted desert crawler.
It's in Blender only and hasn't been exported to Unity yet.

## Brief (the user, 2026-09-24)

- **Size:** compact and walkable, about 22 m long. One walkable deck plus the bridge.
- **Moving parts:** tracks and wheels, doors and hatches, and steering front track units. The searchlights are static.
- **Library parts are used as they are:** the user declined lean variants. The first budget was about 40k triangles; the model is **142k** (breakdown below).
- **Every part is a library component,** browsable in Blender's Asset Browser.

## Layout

- **Bridge:** helm console across the front, pilot and copilot seats, nav station to port, a door aft to the roof deck. Stairwell to starboard.
- **Main deck, forward:** two double bunks, two lockers, a hammock. The stairs go up to starboard: flight, landing, flight.
- **Main deck, amidships:** mess table with two benches, galley stove, the boarding door to port at y −1.1.
- **Engine room** (bulkhead at y 3.5): two V8 diesels flanking a generator. The outer manifolds rise through the roof to two exhaust cowls.
- **Cargo bay** (bulkhead at y 8.2): crates, the rug bundle, a ladder up to the roof hatch, and the stern ramp.
- **Roof:**
  - bridge roof: five searchlights on posts, a whip antenna, a beacon
  - roof deck: side and stern railings, dish and flag masts, the roof hatch, two exhaust cowls
- **Hull:**
  - banded side plating with riveted straps
  - a band of framed side windows (slot, shuttered and barred variations)
  - vented plates beside the engine room
  - a ribbed prow
  - cables slung along both sides

## Collections

The user asked (2026-09-24) for interior and exterior to be separate collections for editing. The generator
still writes one flat `Coll_DuneBarge`; this split was made in the file:

- `Coll_DuneBarge` holds `Arm_DuneBarge`, plus two sub-collections:
  - **Interior:** `_Int_Bridge`, `_Int_CrewQuarters`, `_Int_Mess`, `_Int_EngineRoom`, `_Int_CargoBay`, `_Int_Stairs`, `_Int_Structure` (deck floor, bulkheads).
  - **Exterior:** `_Ext_Hull`, `_Ext_Bridge`, `_Ext_RoofDeck`, `_Ext_HullDressing`, `_Ext_Doors`, and one collection per track unit: `_Ext_Track_MainL` / `_MainR` / `_PodL` / `_PodR`.
- **Boundary pieces:** doors between two interior spaces (engine room, cargo bay) count as interior. The boarding and bridge doors, the hatch and the ramp count as exterior. The deck ceiling is also the roof deck, so it counts as exterior (`_Ext_Hull`).
- **Library copy of the tracks:** the whole starboard running gear is in the library as `components/mechanical/track_assembly.blend` (Main and Pod). It was taken from `dune_barge_track_snapshot.blend`, a snapshot of the live session, so the user's open file didn't have to be saved.

## Collection 2: the user's rebuild

The user is rebuilding the barge by hand in `Collection 2`, with a lower cockpit: floor 5.29, roof top 7.76. Library parts in it are placed at **×1.2**, not ×1.5, so they fit under that ceiling.
- `Coll_Cockpit_Interior` has the helm, pilot, copilot and nav stations, the overhead panel and the dome light.
- `Coll_Cockpit_Roof`:
  - three `floodlight_bank` Single lights along the bow, fanned outward
  - two Sweep lights behind them
  - `antenna_mast` Whip (starboard aft), Beacon (port aft) and Dish
  - about 11.4k triangles

- **Neck** (`neck`, the arch behind the cockpit):
  - It was 32 overlapping clamp-strap ribs, so it z-fought and had open ends. It was rebuilt as one closed shell (`Mesh_Neck_Shell`) by sampling the ribs' inner and outer surfaces.
  - The rib version is kept as `Mesh_DeviceClamp_Strap_Ribs`, and the older uncapped mesh as `Mesh_DeviceClamp_Strap_Backup`. Both have fake users.
- **Stairwell** (`Interior_Stairwell`): the `stair_flight` Grating, re-proportioned to a 32° pitch. It rises from the deck (0.72) to the cockpit floor edge (2.28, y −2.09), with 0.224 m risers on 0.358 m treads.
  - It is 0.98 m wide, because that is the gap between the user's DieselV8 (port) and Inline6 (starboard) in the neck.
  - The player capsule has no step offset, so Unity needs an invisible ramp collider on the pitch line, as the player ship's boarding stair has.
- **Bulkhead** (`Mesh_Neck_Bulkhead`, plus a `Mesh_Neck_DoorFrame_*` frame, in `Exterior_Hull`):
  - It closes the neck's open back end.
  - Its doorway, 1.2 × 3.1 m, is centred on the stair.
  - It stands 5 cm proud of the rim so it clears the engines.

- **Rear hull** (`Exterior_RearHull`), from the neck bulkhead (y 0.30) to the stern (9.42). This is the second version, rebuilt after the user hand-edited the port wall:
  - **Target profile:** the rear ring of the user's port wall, `/ | \`. It runs from x 1.96 at the floor, out to 2.97 at z 2.0, straight up to 3.26, then in to 1.95 at the roof (4.15).
  - **Roof:** the user lowered it to 4.08–4.16. It was left where they put it.
  - **Walls:** both are rebuilt as dense grids, with 32 stations along y and about 36 samples up z. Where the fender and track run, the wall tucks inside them (x ≈ 2.34–2.38). Above them it follows the target width, so the hull overhangs the tracks and sits on the fenders, as in the reference image.
  - **How each station is fitted:** each station measures the highest fender, track or ladder point under the overhang, sampled across the whole span to the neighbouring stations. The wall steps to a ledge 3 cm above that point.
  - **Fender lips:** they sit about 1 cm into the tucked-in lower wall. That is intended.
  - **User's edit:** the hand-edited port wall is kept as mesh `Mesh_RearHull_WallL_UserEdit`, with a fake user.
  - **Stern and collar:** both follow the new profile. They are filled with a constrained triangulation (`bmesh.ops.triangle_fill`). Polyfill had filled the notch at the ledge, and a boolean had wrecked the collar. The collar's neck opening is a proper hole.
- **Hold bulkhead** (`Interior_Hold`) at y 4.0: the inner skin plus 3 cm, with a doorway 1.2 × 3.1 m at x 0.2–1.4. The user put the side hatches at the front, so the hatches open into the forward section.
- **Hatches**, one per side, where the user moved the port hatch (y 0.6–1.8):
  - The sill is raised to 2.32 to clear the curved fender below it. That fender rises from 1.5 at y 0.6 to 2.5 at y 3.6.
  - The top is 3.50, so the opening is 1.2 × 1.18 m. It cuts through the upper slant.
  - Frames and sill are in `Rust_Deep`.
- **Ladders:**
  - **Outside:** the user's `Mesh_Stair_Ladder_FenderL`, at the front of the port track's fender and climbing aft to the hatch, is the reference. It is mirrored to starboard as `Mesh_Stair_Ladder_FenderR`, and both pass through the front of the curved fender.
  - **Inside:** a ladder on the starboard side only (`Mesh_Stair_Ladder_HoldR`). On the port side, the space under the hatch holds the user's jerrican and gas bottles.
- **Dressing** (`dune_barge_c2_dressing.py`). Everything is steel, as for a tank:
  - **Doors:** the plain box frames are replaced by the `hull_door` Bulkhead frame and leaf, fitted to both 1.2 m doorways (×0.545 wide, ×0.85 tall). They are left shut, because open leaves would hit the user's bunk and hammock.
    - The frames' headers run 3 cm into the ceiling. Clear height is about 2.9 m, a little under the 3 m player.
  - **Side hatches:** the `hull_door` Hatch_Roof coaming, at ×0.55, turned onto each wall, with its lid propped open 110°.
  - **Gun deck** (`Interior_GunDeck`):
    - The new `heavy_gun` Autocannon, at ×1.2, sits in the strip aft of the user's paint workshop (y 8.82) and fires aft. Its twin barrels run through a gun port cut in the stern.
    - An armoury against the starboard wall: a `wall_locker` OpenShelf and three upright `launch_tube`s.
    - The gun's parent chain (Base > Cradle > barrels, shield, ammo) is kept, so it can still be rigged.
  - **Stern** (`Exterior_Stern`):
    - A riveted steel mantlet around the gun port. This is the one model-specific piece.
    - A `cable_drum` Winch (×11) on the belly ledge.
    - Six spare `track_link` Grousers bolted either side of the mantlet.
    - `light_fixture` Emergency lamps as tail lights.
    - The `heavy_gun` RocketPod on the rear roof, beside the user's crane and aft of their sail canopy, trained aft.
  - **Side dressing** (`Exterior_SideDressing`): `hull_window` Shuttered, Slot and Barred at ×0.5 on the upper straight band of each wall. They are decorative, with no openings, and the two sides are offset so they don't mirror.
- **New library component:** `components/mechanical/heavy_gun.blend`, with Autocannon, RocketPod and Harpoon. All three are cut as Base (yaw pivot at the mount plate) > Cradle (pitch pivot on the trunnions) > the rest, and authored for 2 m crew.
- **Stern castle** (`dune_barge_c2_castle.py`, collection `Exterior_SternCastle`). The user asked for a taller section at the back with a crown, as in the reference image. The base used to run past the old stern.
  - **Structure:** the old stern wall is gone. The castle runs from y 9.34 to 11.5, over the belly's end. Its / | \ walls are fitted to the tracks like the main walls, but the straight band is 1.04 m taller, so the roof sits at 5.2 against the main roof's 4.16.
  - **Closing parts:** a front step closes it above the main roof, a roof closes the top, and a floor, matching the user's lowered floor (top 0.5), closes the bottom.
  - **Crown:** a crenellated Rust_Heavy parapet on each side and four steel corner posts with spiked caps.
  - **New stern** at y 11.5, with the gun port.
    - The mantlet and spare links moved back and down with the gun.
    - The tail lights moved back.
    - The winch went under the gun port, reduced from ×11 to ×9.
  - **Moves:**
    - the stern Autocannon: its rig bones moved back 2.08 m and down 0.22 m onto the lowered floor
    - the rocket pod: its bones moved onto the castle roof (starboard)
    - the user's crane: moved onto the castle roof (port), where its boom swings its load off the stern
  - **Railings:** library `handrail` Straight at ×1.3 along the main roof edges, gapped around the roof ladders and the user's sail masts and guys. Only the aft stretch survives, about 2.3 m a side.
  - **Helpers:** the wall-fitting code is shared in `dune_barge_c2_hullkit.py`.
- The scripts that made these live edits are saved beside this file as `dune_barge_c2_*.py`. They run only inside the open file through MCP.

Every roof part sits 1 cm into the roof. None of them overlap the user's meshes; this was checked with a BVH test, and the string lights were included in it.

## Rig: `Arm_DuneBarge` (41 bones), rest pose CLOSED

- **`Bone_Chassis`** is the root. Hull, interior, main-track links and main suspension are parented to it.
- **`Bone_Pod_L` and `Bone_Pod_R`** are vertical steering axes. They carry each pod's wheels, links and fender.
- **`Bone_Wheel_<Unit>_<Kind><n>`** for every sprocket, idler, road wheel and return roller. The axle is the bone's Y (along X). It is verified to spin in place.
- **`Bone_Door_Boarding` / `_Engine` / `_Cargo` / `_Bridge`** are vertical hinges. **`Bone_Hatch_Roof` and `Bone_Ramp_Cargo`** are hinges along X.
- **Track links are not boned.** Moving them is a runtime job: step each link along the belt from `dune_barge_tracks.links()`. One bone per link (236 of them) isn't worth it.

## Judgement calls

- **The rounded bridge sits on the bow,** and the rear half is an open roof deck. This follows the image's silhouette, not its exact deck count.
- **The roof hatch is over the cargo bay,** because the engines fill the engine room.
- **The bridge is 4.2 m inside, not 3.4 m,** because door frames are 4.0 m tall.
- **The door frames' 0.15 m sill is the component's own detail,** so wall openings are 3.55 m tall.
- **The main-deck ceiling is also the roof deck and the bridge floor:** one slab, with the stairwell and hatch holes cut in it.
