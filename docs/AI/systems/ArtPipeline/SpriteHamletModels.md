# Sprite hamlet models

Read [../ArtPipeline.md](../ArtPipeline.md) first; the quest itself is [../SpriteHamletQuest.md](../SpriteHamletQuest.md).

**The sprite hamlet** is a knee-high fairy-garden village, the setting of [SpriteHamletQuest](../SpriteHamletQuest.md). **The kit stays at its authored scale in Blender; every hamlet prefab carries ×1.45 on its root** (bellcaps read ~0.9 m, waist height), the NomadSail root-override pattern — so a rigged model is never rescaled in its `.blend`, where location keys would break.

- **Creatures:** two generic-rigged species in `models/creatures/`. `bellcap.blend` is 0.62 m to its hat tip and 0.38 m across the brim, with a bluebell hat and a held acorn on the `Prop_R` bone. `dewling.blend` is 0.57 m, with a glass bubble head. Its glowing spirit hangs on its own `Spirit` bone, so it can float inside the bubble.
- **Creature FBX:** they export to `Creatures/Organic/{Bellcap,Dewling}/` with `Idle` (48 f) and `Walk` (24 f) takes. Controllers are `Animations/Creatures/{Bellcap,Dewling}.controller`, where Idle→Walk is driven by `SpeedY`.
- **Hat colours** are material swaps on the `Bellcap_Hat` renderer, not separate meshes. The swaps are `Materials/Characters/SpriteHamlet/Bellcap_Hat_{Lilac,White}.mat`.
- **Dwellings and props** are two contact sheets: eight dwellings in `models/buildings/sprite_hamlet.blend` and eighteen props in `components/sprite_hamlet/props.blend`. They export one FBX per `Coll_*` to `Environment/Structures/SpriteHamlet/sh_*.fbx` and `Props/SpriteHamlet/sh_*.fbx`.
- **Doors** are 0.72 × 0.42 m, sized to the bellcap's hat and not to the player.
- **Where to see it:** a placed layout is under `SpriteHamlet` in `Tests/Marius test scene.unity`.
- **Variants on the bellcap rig** (copies of `bellcap.blend`, so Idle/Walk carry over): `bellcap_elder.blend` (wilted `Mat_Foliage_Petal_Faded` hat, `Mat_Hide_Beard_MossWhite` beard and brows on Head/Spine, spectacles on the `Head` bone, staff with an amber seed on `Prop_R`, a moss leaf shawl) → `Creatures/Organic/BellcapElder/`, played through `BellcapElder.overrideController`; `sporeling.blend` (a violet puffball cap, `Mat_Hide_Sprite_Withered`, an `Attack` take) → `Creatures/Organic/Sporeling/` with its own `Sporeling.controller`. Both controllers and `Bellcap.controller` carry the full `AgentAnimatorDriver` parameter set.
- **Quest set pieces**, all new files saved by 5.1.0: `models/buildings/sprite_hamlet_quest.blend` (kit scale — Tree of Life, stash lid, bramble hedge, gate arch, well, well spores → `Structures/SpriteHamlet/shq_*`), `models/buildings/witch_glade.blend` (true scale — cauldron, Oath Stone, herb rack and the hut → `Structures/WitchGlade/wg_*`), `components/organic/forest_kit.blend` (true scale — giant mushrooms, gnarled trees, bush, thorn wall, spore puff, shelf-fungus stump, fallen log, sacred and wilted herbs, hat effigy → `Environment/Forest/fk_*`). Contact sheets, one FBX per `Coll_*`.
- **The witch's hut was the library's first texture-painted building** (2026-10-12; the sun shrine followed, [SunShrine.md](../SunShrine.md)): a walk-in hollow tree stump (2.4 × 3.8 m arched door hung open, three round windows) under a curled, patched witch hat with chimney, lanterns, roots, toadstools and a moss skirt. One mesh `wg_witch_hut` (the prefab's MeshCollider names it), 7.6k tris, two materials: local `Mat_WitchHut` — a 4096² atlas baked from procedural bark/planks/stone/fabric/moss shaders × baked AO — and palette `Mat_Emissive_Amber` (panes, lantern glow). Master texture `_Source~/models/buildings/textures/wg_witch_hut_BaseColor.jpg`; the export copies it to `wg_witch_hut.fbm/`, which is what Unity binds (the Vrescal layout) — repaint the JPG, then re-export. Pre-rebuild file: `_backups~/witch_glade_before_hut_2026-10-12.blend`.
- **The witch's clothes** are `Clothes_Witch*` garments on the Raxy rig in `drifters/morwen.blend`, exported alone (rig + garments, `scale_all`, `triangulate`) to `Characters/Morwen/morwen.fbx` (Generic) with materials remapped to `Materials/Characters/Raxy/Raxy_Witch*.mat`; `DrifterClothes.EnsurePrefabs` made their prefabs in `Raxy/Clothes/` ([CharacterClothes.md](../CharacterClothes.md)). `morwen.blend` is a copy of `raxy.blend` saved by Blender **5.1.0** — the reason it is a separate file (see Gotchas). Her three hat herbs are not part of it: they are `fk_sacred_herb` instances on the `Head` bone in `Morwen.prefab`, hidden one by one as the fight burns them ([SpriteHamletQuest.md](../SpriteHamletQuest.md)).
- **The Oathbranch** (`models/gear/oathbranch.blend`, Blender 5.1.0, 2026-10-12, built headless) → `Items/oathbranch.fbx`: three bark strands twisted round a driftwood pole that open into a cage round an `Orb`, a few leaves, and a `Muzzle` empty at the orb centre (exported with `keep_empties=True`). The orb's palette `Mat_Emissive_Portal_Blue` is replaced on the prefab by `Materials/Effects/StasisOrb.mat`, whose `_Appear` the artifact drives as its recharge.
- **The witch's thorn** (`components/organic/thorn_spike.blend`, built headless 2026-10-13) → `Environment/Forest/thorn_spike.fbx`: one curved bark thorn, 1 m tall with its origin at the base, two side barbs, moss-green tip. The FBX root is ×100 and −90° X like every library export, so `Prefabs/VisualEffects/Thorns/ThornSpike.prefab` wraps it under a plain root that `ThornEruption` scales.
