# Colony interiors

## Model
The `Colony_*` buildings are walked into directly: no scene load. Each hull is hollow (cut in `mars_colony.blend`, see
[ArtPipeline.md](ArtPipeline.md)), lined with an interior shell, and **every room has at most one exterior airlock**
(2026-10-04, v5): a room's other exterior doors are sealed. Windows and the hub domes are real glass: from inside you see
the desert and the sky, from outside the lit rooms.

### Airlock effects
The cycle is told as physics. **Venting**: the pressure drop chills the chamber air below its dew point, so it flashes into condensation fog that streams up into the two high vent intakes. **Pressurising**: white jets roar out of the two low nozzles, slow and billow out, and a fog rises off the floor and fills the chamber; at rest with room air a thin floor mist stays. Everything lives inside the two airlock prefabs, so all 13 airlocks share it.

| Layer (on each airlock prefab) | What it is |
|---|---|
| `VentJet_1/2` (on the high `VentNozzle_1/2`) | Intake streaks: a 0.9 m hemisphere shell emitting **inward** (negative start speed), stretched billboards that die at the nozzle |
| `FillJet_1/2` (on the low `VentNozzle_3/4`) + child `Core` | The billow (cone 14°, 7-10 m/s, linear drag 5 so it stops within ~2 m, growing ×5) and its fast stretched white core (0.12-0.2 s) |
| `Mist/VentFog` | The flash fog: a box over the chamber, big translucent puffs, emission shaped by `ventFogEnvelope` (thick at once, nothing new after half the cycle) |
| `ChamberHaze` (moved to the floor) | Fog rising off a thin floor box, rate scaled by pressurising progress, `restingHaze` at rest |
| `Mist/Intake_1/2` | `ParticleSystemForceField`s (sphere, range 3.5 m, gravity + vortex + drag) at the intakes, **enabled only while venting**, listed in `VentFog`'s and `ChamberHaze`'s External Forces |
| `Mist/Bounds/*` | Six plane transforms on the chamber's measured faces (up = into the chamber); **every** mist system collides with them. `Ceiling_Fog` (1 m under `Ceiling`) replaces the ceiling for `VentFog` and `ChamberHaze`, whose cards are up to ~2 m wide |

Materials: `AirlockMist.mat` (jets, `_Erosion` 0.7) and `AirlockFog.mat` (fog layers, `_Erosion` 0.15: eroded at low alpha a fog puff shrinks to a hard core, the "cotton ball" look), both [`SixWayMist.shader`](Assets/Game/Art/Shaders/Effects/SixWayMist.shader): a six-way lit flipbook (the light's direction in the card's own frame picks and weights six baked lightmaps, so a lamp lights the side facing it and glows through from behind; the Forward+ cluster of point/spot lights, so the amber beacon sweeps through the fog; the sun is scaled by `_MainLightGain`, **0 on both airlock materials**), 64-frame blending, alpha erosion blended with fade, soft particles against the depth texture (on in both URP assets already) and a near-camera fade. The flipbook is baked by [`tools/mist_flipbook.py`](tools/mist_flipbook.py) (raymarched billow volume, Beer-Lambert transmittance along each axis; output `Art/Textures/Effects/AirlockMist_*`, imported linear, BC7, clamp).

Why built this way: real-time smoke that holds up close is a lit, frame-blended flipbook softened where it meets geometry (Unity's six-way smoke, soft particles, alpha erosion), and the cycle is the airlock's one wait, so it gets light, motion and sound together (`GDC-L1-FEEL-0004`); the fog is exaggerated for readability rather than simulated (`GDC-L1-FEEL-0007`), and the particle budget is fixed per airlock (`GDC-L1-PERF-0004`). A `FogVolume` pulse was rejected: the volumetric fog marches at most 8 volumes per frame for the whole world, and 13 airlocks would compete for them. VFX Graph was not used: its six-way pipeline expects Houdini/EmberGen bakes and compute, and Shuriken + one shader already covers both renderers.

## Flows
1. **Coming in.** Right-click the outer hatch ("RMB: vent and open") -> the chamber vents (`cycleSeconds`, 3 s: jets, beacon, amber flashing, klaxon) -> the outer hatch swings out -> step in -> right-click the inner hatch: refused, "Close the outer hatch first" -> right-click the outer hatch shut behind you -> right-click the inner hatch -> the chamber pressurises (haze fills it) -> the inner leaves slide apart. Nothing closes on its own.
2. **Leaving.** Right-click the inner hatch ("RMB: open") -> it opens at once (the chamber holds room air) -> step in, close it behind you -> right-click the outer hatch -> vent -> it opens.
3. **Interlock.** A hatch never opens while the other is not fully shut. The clicker stands in the chamber (`chamber` zone, read on their own machine and sent in the request) -> they can reach the other hatch, so they are told to close it. They stand outside the chamber (the deadlock: someone left the far hatch open) -> the far hatch is sealed for them, unless someone stands in its doorway ("Someone is standing in the inner hatch"). A hatch is never told to shut on someone in its own doorway ("Step clear of the hatch first"). Every click during a cycle is refused ("The airlock is cycling"). Why it is built this way: every press is answered, by a move or by a message saying why not, and the crosshair prompt says beforehand what the press will do (`GDC-L1-UX-0003`); the lamps, haze and readout show which air the chamber holds before anyone clicks (`GDC-L1-SYS-0006`); the cycle, the one slow event the player waits on, is layered across sight, sound and camera (`GDC-L1-FEEL-0004`), with the kick small, local and on the shake setting (`GDC-L1-FEEL-0006`).
4. **Pressure.** The chamber holds one side's air (`Vented`). A hatch on that side opens at once; the other side waits for both hatches to report fully shut, then runs `Venting` or `Pressurising` for `cycleSeconds`, then opens. The readout's bar is the cycle's progress and its value text the chamber's air.
5. **One click, end to end.** `Interactor` -> `AirlockHatch.Interact` -> `AirlockChamber.Operate` checks with the state this machine already holds; a refusal is a visor message (`PlayerHints`, id `airlock`) + `InteractDenied` here and nothing is sent -> otherwise `NetMsg.AirlockOperate` -> the server re-checks (`AirlockCycle.Operate`, doorway zones read there), ticks the cycle in `Update`, and on every change `NetMsg.AirlockState` to all -> every machine moves the leaves; `AirlockPressureEffects` reads `AirlockChamber.State`.

## Multiplayer
- **The airlock is server-decided.** It used to be presence-driven and send nothing; a click is a player's choice, which only one machine can be allowed to decide. `NetMsg.AirlockOperate` (124, clicker -> server; `B` bit 0 = outer, bit 1 = from the chamber, `-1` = ask) and `NetMsg.AirlockState` (125, server -> everyone; `B` = `AirlockState.ToWire()`, bit 8 = instant) ride the building's channel, `A` = `NetChannel.IndexOf(chamber)` (positional, like latches). The server's handler and tick are gated on `Network.Simulates(chamber)`; every handler is idempotent on the state, because the host hears its own announcement and a joiner's answer goes to everyone.
- **Late joiners and streamed-in chunks** ask once the wrapper is spawned (`NetToServerWhenSpawned`) and land in the answer instantly and silently; the effects pick a running cycle up from its start.
- **Where the channel comes from.** `SettlementNetworking.Wrap` puts a `NetworkObject` + `NetRelay` round any generated building holding an `AirlockChamber` (as for a `NetworkBehaviour` or a latch). **A colony placed by hand has none: every machine then runs its own airlock** (a client's click opens the hatch on that client only), and `NetChannel.WarnUnrelayed` logs `[Net] '<root>' handled message 124 locally` once per session. Place colonies through a settlement, or give the building a scene `NetworkObject` + `NetRelay` the way `Wrap` does.
- **Refusals the server makes are silent.** The clicker's machine already refused anything its replicated state forbids; a click that raced somebody else's (two players, one hatch) is dropped on the server with no message.
- The oxygen filler and gear wall are `NetworkBehaviour`s with no `NetworkObject` of their own: `SettlementNetworking.Wrap` now also wraps any building holding a `NetworkBehaviour`, and they use that wrapper. A colony placed by hand, outside a generated settlement, has no wrapper: the filler and the wall then only work offline and for the host.
- **Wall and board stock is server-authoritative.** Every machine lays the authored starting gear when the wall is built, and the server's layout (restored from the save, then changed by takes) reaches clients through `WallInventoryNetwork`'s list, which a client adopts on spawn and on every change. Nothing loose is spawned in the colony: no item `NetworkObject` is ever nested in a building.
- **The display suit and the rover parts are scenery**: no `NetworkObject`, no `Rigidbody`, no script, the same bytes on every machine. A sealed door is scenery too: no hatch, no chamber, nothing sent.
- **Removing an airlock renumbers the rest.** `NetChannel.IndexOf(chamber)` is positional on the building, so sealing a door shifts the indices of the chambers after it. Harmless: every machine builds the same prefab and gets the same numbers, and nothing saves or keys on a chamber's index (the airlock state is not saved).
- **Not run on a client, nor in a generated settlement.** The clicked airlock (2026-10-03) has not been run in a Play session at all yet, host or client: it compiles and `AirlockCycleTests` cover the decisions. The ×1.4 rebuild (2026-10-04) is verified in the editor only: 282 physics probes over the seven prefabs (door rays meet `OuterHatch_Door`, floors and ramps at height, a 3 m capsule through every tube and every airlock with its hatches open), wiring checks (every chamber's hatches, zones and effects set; no missing scripts), and preview-scene renders with the jets and haze simulated by hand.

## Persistence
| State | Where |
|---|---|
| Oxygen filler | `OxygenGeneratorSaveable`; an untouched plant saves nothing, so `startingBattery` re-seeds a battery someone removed |
| Gear wall, small gear boards | `WallInventorySaveable` through the entity `PersistentFixture` earns it; every board and wall in a building is told apart positionally (`WallIndex`), so a re-laid building that adds, removes or reorders boards hands an old save's records to different boards (v5 moved the boards in `HabPod`, `HabRow` MD and `DockPod`; a world saved before it may show a board's gear on its neighbour). A wall authored with starting gear writes its record even when empty (an empty `placements` list), so gear a player took is not laid on again at the next load; a wall built empty still writes nothing |
| Projector on/off | `ProjectorSaveable` |
| Hatches, airlock | **Not saved, on purpose.** On load (and whenever a chunk reloads or the server restarts the building) every hatch is shut and every chamber holds the room's air, nothing pending: the state a fresh `AirlockCycle` starts in. A hatch somebody left open is shut again, which is safe: nothing else reads it (it is not a `SandstormShelter` door) |

**Not verified by a save/reload in Play.** The empty-record rule is covered by `WallInventoryTests.AWallEmptiedOfItsStartingGearStaysEmptyAfterAReload`; that the record is restored after `Awake` has laid the starting gear on a chunk hydrate (`RestoreAuthored` runs after the scene loads) is read from the code, not run.

## Gotchas
- **From inside, the hull's faces are back faces.** The liner must enclose every room completely; any gap shows the desert through the "wall". That is also exactly why the glass works.
- **A Boolean into a closed hull makes a blind pocket.** The room volume is subtracted first (hollowing the hull), then doorways cut through the remaining shell. Cut a doorway into an un-hollowed hull and a cap face blocks it.
- **Export moves the cutters too** (`mars_colony_export.py`); `export_collections` would leave them behind and cut every doorway in the wrong place.
- **FBX axes: Unity = (-x, z, -y) of Blender.** A layout read off Blender without the flip lands every door on the wrong side of a non-symmetric building.
- **The gear wall needs a 5.6 m run of clear wall**, and after the ×1.4 every module wall run is at most 4.3 m (window, airlock and doorway take the rest). It therefore stands 1.25 m off a sealed stub's end wall (clear of the sealed hatch), in a hub or in `DockPod`'s stub end; `HabRow` and `HabPod` have no such wall and get small gear boards instead.
- **The stairs are visual only**: 0.65-0.8 m rises, the lowest 1.37 m above the ground, and the player has no step offset. `StairsRamp_n` is a 19.9° slab from the sill at the hull face to the ground 7.8 m out, so it floats over the back of each step, sinks into the front of the next, and runs on over the sand for its last 3.8 m.
- **The outer hatch's rim (2.766 m) is lower than the sill (2.828 m)**: the disc overlaps the r 2.38 doorway by 0.14 m. A stair top at the sill would be swept by the opening hatch, so the stairs stand 10 cm low.
- **Surfaces 1.5 cm apart, in a fixed order, are what stopped the z-fighting** (in `mars_colony.blend`): from the room outward, doorway collar < liner wall hole < hull hole, window reveal < hull window cut < wall hole, dome collar < hull dome cut < ceiling hole, all on the cutters' own polygon angles; and no two liner boxes share a plane. Props keep the same rule: every floor prop stands 2 cm off the floor (the floor's light stripe is at +1.2 cm), floor grates 2.5 cm, wall-backed props 2 cm off the wall or 19 cm where they overlap a 0.168 m wall rib.
- **Tubes were shredded into slats by an exact Boolean of a bore sitting inside the hull skin's own facets.** The 28-sided bore (r 2.63) is now clear of the 16-sided skin and its groove; the necks narrower than a doorway are closed from outside by a `Mat_Colony_Hull` sleeve on the tube liner (culled from inside). The tube's walkable floor is a strip only 1.64 m wide; the rest is the curve, approximated in `Colliders` by a 42.6° slab the player slides off.
- **Blind faces (stub / nothing) end in a liner plate 3 cm behind the wall face**, so the `SealedHatch` hangs in front of it, never coplanar.
- **The vestibule's back edges follow an 8.25 m arc that reaches 0.76 m BEHIND its origin**, but the hub wall is flat: on the wall plane the side walls would poke 0.3 m out through the hull. It stands 0.38 m into the hub, which buries the arc in the 0.42 m hull wall and leaves a slot (≤ 0.38 m) between its ceiling and the wall above the doorway.
- **A single-node FBX imports with its mesh on the root**, rotated (270, 0, 0): the `Model` instance keeps that rotation; turning it means `Quaternion * originalRotation` (as on the gear board), never a fresh Euler.
- **A hatch leaf needs a SOLID collider on the same GameObject as its `AirlockHatch`** (both shipped hatch kinds have a `BoxCollider` there): a trigger is see-through to the `Interactor`, and a collider on a child with no `AirlockHatch` above it blocks the crosshair instead of answering it.
- **Every leaf must be in its chamber's `inner` or `outer` array.** `AirlockChamber.Awake` binds them; an unbound leaf is a solid wall that offers no prompt. A missing hatch array or zone logs `[Airlock] ... is missing a hatch or a zone` - and a missing doorway zone is how a hatch would shut on a player.
- **The `chamber` zone decides "told" versus "sealed for you".** It is read on the clicker's machine, where their own body is the truth; if it does not cover the whole floor between the hatches, a player standing in the uncovered strip gets the far hatch slammed for them instead of the "close it first" message.
- **Dead wiring from the presence-driven airlock:** `Zone_Outside_n` / `Zone_RoomApproach` objects (and their `PresenceZone`s) and the chamber's old `roomApproach`, `outsideApproach`, `closeDelaySeconds`, `lampRenderers`, `statusLight` fields no longer exist in code; the lamps and status light are wired on `AirlockPressureEffects` now.
- **`AirlockPressureEffects` only scales what each particle system was authored with** (`rateOverTimeMultiplier` captured in `Awake`): a jet authored with zero emission stays invisible. Jets want `Play On Awake` off and `Looping` on; the haze `Looping` on (it is started by the script).
- **The jets hang off FBX nozzle nodes whose world scale is 100** (the FBX's centimetre nodes). With the default `Scaling Mode` Hierarchy every jet was drawn 100× too big: 35-85 m billboards (clamped to half the screen by `Max Particle Size`) flying ~1 km, straight through the hull — the "visible from outside, rectangular and fake" airlock. **Every mist system is `Scaling Mode` Local.** Any new particle system under an FBX node needs the same, or a non-scaled parent.
- **Containment is the `Mist/Bounds` planes, not lifetimes.** A plane's up is its collision normal and must point into the chamber; the planes sit on the chamber's measured faces (bulkhead: x ±3.91, y 0.05-5.26, z -2.95…-0.21; vestibule: x ±3.0, y 0.09-5.48, z -0.33…3.30, airlock-local). Move or resize an airlock's walls and these planes go with them. Fog layers have `Lifetime Loss` **0**: a 3 m puff born near the floor touches the floor plane every frame, and any loss kills it within a second — the haze simply never appeared. **`Radius Scale` is 0.01: the planes hold particle CENTRES only.** With a real collision radius, a puff that grows (Size over Lifetime) while pressed on a plane is ejected by the solver — kilometres away within a second, drawn as huge white streaks by the stretched systems. Soft particles hide the card crossing the wall instead.
- **Lit mist shows every light leak.** With the sun on, the vestibule's fog drew stepped bright bands: the main light's shadow map does not seal the chamber, so sunlight reached the cards through a ceiling that casts no shadow. Indoor mist uses `_MainLightGain` 0. The beacon's spot needs a soft edge (`innerSpotAngle` 8° of 50°): a hard cone draws a crisp curved band across every card.
- **`SixWayMist` reads vertex streams in a fixed order: Position, Normal, Colour, UV, UV2, AnimBlend, Tangent.** A system with a different list draws garbage or nothing, silently. The Normal + Tangent streams are the card's lighting frame; Texture Sheet Animation must be on (8 × 8, whole sheet) for UV2/AnimBlend to mean anything.- **`openEuler` is applied in the hatch node's own frame**, and an FBX node carries the Blender-to-Unity rotation (270, 0, 0): the outer hatch opens outward with (0, 0, -100), not the (0, -100, 0) a reading of "yaw about Unity up" gives. Measure the disc centre after the rotation before trusting a sign.
- **There is no hiss or airlock event in the FMOD catalog** (the Studio project is lost): the cycle borrows `ShipAlarm` (klaxon), `InteractOxygenFillLoop` (hiss) and `InteractOxygenFilled` (valve thud) as stand-ins.
- Research, crafting and repair are still only the loops colonists play at the research desk, rock analysis station, rover bay and workbench: no mechanic sits behind them (the bunks are slept in, see Colonists). Stools, chairs and benches are real `Seat`s, with no solid collider.
- **A wall with starting gear must save even when empty.** `WallInventory.Awake` lays the authored starting lists on every build, including every load; only a record clears them. Before 2026-10-04 `WallInventorySaveable` wrote nothing for an empty wall, so an emptied board (and the ship's battery) came back after every reload.
- **The display suit stays in its bind pose.** The suit stand's posts (±1.33 m) and yoke are sized for it; an idle clip sampled into the bones hunches it and staggers its feet, and an arms-wider pose clips the posts. `BakeMesh` on its renderers does not follow the posed bones in edit mode (the bounds it returns are the bind pose's), so measure a posed rig by its bones, never by a baked mesh.
- **The rover parts copy `Rover.prefab`'s renderers, not its FBXs.** Each part is a set of renderer paths in the rover (`Body/Appendage/`, `LegL/Joint1/BogieL1/`, single `Body/Body/Cube.1xx` nodes...) copied into one `Model` scaled 0.4. A re-modelled rover does not reach them: rebuild the parts. The rover's chassis is one closed box (`Body/Body/Cube`), so "insides exposed" is the body's inner components laid out on their own (`Electronics`, `PowerModule`) beside the empty shell, with harnesses spilling out.
- **Z-fighting in the colony was placement, measured, not guessed** (2026-10-04, a coplanar-triangle scan of every `Colony_*` prefab with the hatches shut and open). The causes, now fixed: (1) **the gear wall was placed at 1.44, not 1.2** — the planner multiplied a 1.2 scale into a prefab whose root already carries 1.2, so the wall was 6.7 m wide and 5.9 m tall, poked 0.13-0.4 m through the ceiling, swallowed the cabinets beside it (their faces inside its frame) and drew its gear grid 17 % smaller than its bays; (2) **the small gear board hung over the bulkhead's -x panel**, whose room status lamp and gauge knobs (to +0.242) went through it and whose face features shared its back plane; (3) **straight pipe runs (4.006 m) on a 4.0 m pitch** overlapped 6 mm end to end; (4) **floor mats laid on the floor** put their underside 2 mm from the floor's light stripe (+1.2 cm); (5) the emergency station's back sat on a +0.182 panel face; (6) a crate stood on two floor grates; (7) the outer hatch's hinge mount was coplanar with `HabPod.door0`'s face panel. **The rule: no two placed pieces within 3 mm of one plane over more than 0.5 cm²**, which `ColonyZFightTests` enforces. Coplanar faces INSIDE one model (a label flush with its panel, the airlock leaves' edge seal flush with the leaf edge — exposed only when the leaves slide open, the "open door glitch") are the model's to fix and were fixed in `astronaut_decorations.blend` v5 (every detail ≥ 5 mm proud). Back-to-back faces (a bulkhead's edge against the liner) never draw together with culling on, so they do not count unless a material is two-sided.
- **Hull z-fights are the hull export's** ([ArtPipeline.md](ArtPipeline.md)): a cutter shared by hull and trim leaves coincident caps (trims use their own `.trimN` / `.insetN` cutter copies), the hub wall rings are mostly cut away by the hollowing, and a per-FBX scan's "internal" pairs are mostly hidden caps — judge them with rays, plugs hidden as Unity hides them. **A dome ring needs two cutters:** its `.inset1` cut face sits in front of the hull's dome-hole wall above the roof (sealing against the liner's dome collar) but must sit behind it below the roof top, so `Large.H1` / `TwinDome.H2` rings carry a second `<hub>.domecut.ringbase` cutter (the dome cut grown 2 cm, topped at the roof). Left after v5: the gear wall's own end caps inside its jambs (not visible).
- **A door plug squashed along its axis keeps its outside look.** The sealed doors reuse the hull's plug node: `localScale` on the node axis that lies along the door (measured, never assumed: FBX nodes carry the (270, 0, 0) import rotation) times `k`, then moved so the outer face stays put. Unsquashed, a module plug reached 0.79 m and a hub plug 1.2 m into the room, straight through the `SealedHatch`.
- **A sealed door must look different from a working one.** An `OuterHatch` with its `AirlockHatch` removed was rendered beside the plug and passed over: identical to a working door, it invites a click that does nothing (`GDC-L1-UX-0003`: every press is answered). The hull's own wheel-locked door, with no stairs below it, reads as shut.
- **The 4.7 m bunk does not fit beside a module window** (the runs are 4.31 m), so bunks stand in hubs; `HabRow` MD's overlaps 0.21 m of the window's clear opening.
- **`execute_code` history is shared with other sessions**: never `replay` an index, paste the command again.

## Extending
- **A new prop:** add a `Coll_AstroDeco_<Name>` (root at its ground centre = `instance_offset`, front -Y, one `<Name>_Body` mesh, moving parts as their own nodes) to `astronaut_decorations.blend`, run `astronaut_decorations_export.py`, wrap it as `AstroDeco_<Name>.prefab` (FBX instance as `Model`, `Colliders/Box` from the bbox, `SettlementFixture`, static 94 except moving parts and seats; a seat gets a `Seat` per sitting place at the seat top with `footDrop` = its height and a trigger, no solid collider). Rebuild an existing one in place (`LoadPrefabContents` → clear → `SaveAsPrefabAsset`) so its GUID, and every colony that nests it, survives.
- **Re-export the hulls:** `blender --background --python Assets/Game/Art/Models/_Source~/models/buildings/mars_colony_export.py`. The FBX GUIDs and door node names must not change. A moved door, room or window means re-laying the building.
- **Re-laying a building (2026-10-03, ×1.4; re-run 2026-10-04 for v4 with the bigger furniture, the clutter kit, a fill pass that lines free wall runs and hangs wall pieces, and the rover bay):** a throw-away planner, not a checked-in builder. A Python pass read A's room survey (per-room liner walls, windows, doorways, ribs, door centres) and B's prop bounding boxes, packed wall-backed props from each room's corners inward (never crossing a window, doorway, airlock or another prop's footprint, never into the walking lanes from every hatch and doorway), and wrote one line per object; a `LoadPrefabContents` pass executed the lines into each `Colony_*` (root GameObject kept, so `Chunk_5_3`'s instances stay attached; every child rebuilt). Its checks: no prop overlaps another or a lane, every prop inside its room with ≥ 1.5 cm to the liner, 286 physics probes per run (v4: 0 failed; the floor probes read the shell's own `Under_`/`TubeFloor_` boxes under whatever stands on the spot, and a capsule walks round the rover) (door rays meet `OuterHatch_Door` at three heights, floors and ramps at the right height, a 3 m capsule through every tube and every airlock with its hatches open).
- **v5 (2026-10-04) re-ran the planner** with one airlock per room (a `SEALED` set of door nodes: their faces become shut wall in the shell colliders, a `SealedHatch` inside and the squashed plug outside, `S` lines in the plan), the rover parts bay, the gear wall at its prefab scale and the bulkhead panel / pipe / mat offsets above. Its checks gained lane rings (a 2 m keep-out round every table and the hoist), capsule walks through the rover bay lanes, and a flood fill per room (0.25 m grid, a 3 m capsule r 0.45, from inside the room's way in): v5 result 278 probes, 0 failed; every room reachable except pockets of 1-2 m² behind furniture; 0 cables in a chamber; 13 chambers fully wired; no `ventTarget` left in any `Colony_*`.
- **Z-fight guard:** `ColonyZFightTests.NoTwoPlacedPiecesShareAVisibleSurface` instantiates every `Colony_*` prefab in a preview scene, gathers every visible triangle in world space (skinned renderers baked), hatches shut and then open (each `AirlockHatch` moved by its `openOffset` / `openEuler`), bins them by plane and fails on any coplanar (normals within 0.999, every vertex within 3 mm of the other's plane), same-facing, overlapping (> 0.5 cm²) pair that belongs to two different placed pieces (the outermost prefab instance below the building). It lists the worst pairs by path and area. Placing anything new in a colony: run it.
- **Airlock timing:** `AirlockCycle.cycleSeconds` (on each `AirlockChamber`), each `AirlockHatch.travelSeconds`, and the effects' `jetEnvelope` and `ventFogEnvelope` over the cycle. The mist's look is tuned on the particle systems in the two airlock prefabs and on the two materials; to change the puff itself, edit and re-run `python3 tools/mist_flipbook.py` (writes straight into `Art/Textures/Effects/`).
- **Wiring an airlock:** the two airlock prefabs already carry `AirlockChamber` (`inner` = the two leaves; `chamber`, `innerDoorway`, `outerDoorway` = the `Zones/` trigger boxes, all `PresenceZone`, layer Default) and `AirlockPressureEffects` (jets, haze, beacon light + rotor, lamp renderers, status light, `DamageShake`). Their zones assume the placement rule above (bulkhead 3.0 m in from the end wall; vestibule 0.38 m in front of the hub wall), so move a zone with the airlock. Placing one, link `outer` = the doorway's `OuterHatch_n/Model/OuterHatch_Door` hatch; the effects need no link. Each leaf: `AirlockHatch` + a solid collider on the same GameObject. The mist is described under [Airlock effects](#airlock-effects); vent intakes are the two high nozzles, fill jets the two low ones. `ventTarget` (the old pull toward the outer hatch) is gone: the 18 nested airlocks in the `Colony_*` prefabs may still carry a stale `ventTarget` override, which Unity ignores ("Remove unused overrides" clears it).
- **Another board size:** keep `displayScale` at the wall's 1.8171431 so gear is drawn the size it is on the wall and in the world (`ItemWorldScale`), and author the face as whole cells (`PackGrid.Cell` 0.0945 logical = 0.1717 m drawn). Measure what it holds against `PackShapes.For(item, library)` (battery 7 × 3, oxygen tank 3 × 6, both turnable) before shipping it.
