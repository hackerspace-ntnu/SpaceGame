# Desert monowheel — build record

`desert_monowheel.blend`, from the concept sheet "Desert monowheel" (designs 1 and 2). The Runner was generated by `desert_monowheel.py` and then finished by hand. The Hauler and Patched are copies of that finished Runner, made by [`desert_monowheel_variants.py`](desert_monowheel_variants.py).

## Variations

| Collection | What it is | Exported? |
|---|---|---|
| `Coll_Monowheel_Runner` | Sheet design 1, hand-finished by the user. **The master. Nothing may modify it.** | yes |
| `Coll_Monowheel_Hauler` | The Runner plus luggage: the rug-wrapped bundle, bedroll and lashing on the ski (forward, clear of the cheek rails' feet), one trade-goods sack on the rear rails behind the seat, and a saddlebag on each side hoop (canvas with a bedroll on the left, leather with two canisters on the right). Built by [`monowheel_luggage.py`](monowheel_luggage.py), shared with the Double. | yes |
| `Coll_Monowheel_Patched` | The Runner, worn: rust patch plates bolted to the ring (they spin with it), mismatched paddle boards (two silvered timber, one steel), board 05 torn off leaving its bare mount, a rusted plate riveted over the ski deck, and a tattered canvas pennant on a mast above the tail post. | yes |

All three sit at the origin. Hauler and Patched are hidden in the viewport, so use the eye icon to switch between them.

**The previous Hauler and Patched were deleted on 2026-09-24.** They were the generator's unfinished twin-ring and sail versions, and never got the user's hand edits. That's why `desert_monowheel.py` still describes them.

**Rebuilding them.** `desert_monowheel_variants.py` refuses to run once they exist. To rebuild after changing the Runner, delete the two collections and run it again:

```
blender --background models/vehicles/desert_monowheel.blend --python-expr "import bpy; exec(open(r'models/vehicles/desert_monowheel_variants.py').read()); bpy.ops.wm.save_mainfile()"
```

**Gotchas from building them:**
- **Never raise `SystemExit` in a script run inside a live Blender.** It quits Blender with no prompt and loses every unsaved change. It's fine in `--background` generators; in-session guards raise `RuntimeError`.
- **A just-built object reads an identity `matrix_world` until `view_layer.update()`.** Parenting it before that drops it at the origin.
- **The side hoops' straight run has cross-sections only at y = 0.84 and 1.20.** At 1.48 it bends up toward the tail. A station measured between 1.20 and 1.48 interpolates into the bend, and `rail_at` refuses it.

## What spins

- `Arm_Monowheel_<V>` has two bones:
  - `Bone_Chassis` at the origin.
  - `Bone_Ring` on the hub (0, 0, 1.95), pointing +X. Rotating it about its own Y axis spins the wheel, and +X rotation rolls the vehicle forward (−Y).
- The ring band (`Mesh_Ring_<V>`, or `Mesh_RingL_Hauler` on the Hauler) is parented to `Bone_Ring`. Its origin is on the hub and its rotation is identity. Every other spinning part is parented to the band, so rotating the band object alone also spins the whole wheel:
  - paddle boards and their iron mounts
  - the Hauler's second ring and tie bars
  - the Patched variation's patch plates
- Everything static is parented to `Bone_Chassis`: rollers, frame, seat, ski, tail and cargo.
- The paddles are the traction. The iron lip on each board's leading edge is the scoop that bites the sand. It's placed for forward rolling: on the bottom paddle it faces +Y.
- `Socket_Rider_<V>` is an empty on the seat cushion, where the seated player attaches.

## The Runner has been hand-edited, and the .blend is now the source of truth

The Runner was reworked by hand in Blender (2026-09-24), so **do not regenerate it** from `desert_monowheel.py`. Its geometry no longer matches the script:

- **The armature is tilted.** `Arm_Monowheel_Runner` is rotated about −16° around X, and the ring moved to a hub at world (0, 0.543, 1.873). `Bone_Ring` was re-seated on that hub, in *armature* space. Anyone moving the ring again must move `Bone_Ring`'s head with it. A bone left on the old hub makes the wheel orbit instead of spin, and nothing warns you.
- **Hand-added static parts, all parented to `Bone_Chassis`:**
  - `Mesh_BellyPan` (the rust box under the seat)
  - `Mesh_FrontCowl`
  - `Mesh_FootPedalL` and `Mesh_FootPedalR`
  - `Mesh_SideHoopL` and `Mesh_SideHoopR`
  - `Mesh_RearRailL` and `Mesh_RearRailR`
  - `Mesh_TailPanel`
  - `Mesh_TailPost`, with `Mesh_TailPostCapLow` and `Mesh_TailPostCapHigh`
- **Removed from the Runner:** the spine rails, the tail mount, and the generated fin and fin trim.
- `Mesh_FrontCowl_Runner` and `Mesh_SkiScrolls_Runner` use a local material called `Material`, not the palette.

## Decomposition

Nothing is merged. Parts are one object each: the ring band and its side bolts, each paddle board, each paddle mount (bracket, crossbar and lip), each roller, each roller's arms, the lower and rear cross tubes, the left and right straight spine rails, the left and right cheek frames (posts rising from the spine) with their scrollwork, the seat pan, seat cushion, backrest, back cushion, seat posts, tiller, grips, footrest, tail mount, tail fin, fin trim, ski deck, ski rails, straps, runners and ski scrolls. The Hauler adds the cargo bundle, rug, rug border, fringe, lashing, bedroll and bedroll ties.

No existing library components were reused. The nearest candidates were `components/props/crew_seat` (upholstered cockpit seats, which are the wrong language for a wrought-iron sand sled) and `components/mechanical/road_wheel` (a tyred wheel, not a rim ring). The monowheel parts are local to this file rather than new component files, because nothing else in the library uses a rim-driven ring yet. Split them out if a second ring vehicle shows up.

## Geometry constraints worth knowing before editing

- **The ring can't be crossed.** The seat cradle rides the ring on three flanged rollers inside it, at the front-bottom, rear-bottom and rear-top. It reaches the outside only *sideways*: the cross tubes run out to the cheek frames at ±`cheek` (0.44 m, or 0.84 m on the Hauler).
- **The chassis spine is one straight diagonal rail per side, as on the sheet.** It leaves the ski 0.9 m ahead of the ski's rear end, climbs past the wheel at seat height, and carries on in the same line up to the tail panel clamp at (y = 2.9 m, z = hub + 0.75 m). The cross tubes that carry the cradle hang off it at y = −0.35 m and y = 1.0 m, so their heights follow the line (`spine_z`). A braced truss with scrollwork ties it down to the ski's rear, and a strut runs up to the panel's top clamp. It stays at x = ±`cheek`, outside the paddle sweep (|x| < board edge, radius < 1.97 m about the hub). The ski deck stops about 1 m in front of the hub for the same reason. On the Hauler the ski is 1.72 m wide, so its rails meet the spine at ±0.84 m.
- **Hub at 1.95 m**, with paddle tips touching z = 0. The seat pan is at hub − 0.45 m. The seat is 0.9 m wide with a 1.25 m backrest standing nearly upright (5° recline, as the user asked), sized for the 3 m player *seated*. The inner band clears the rider's head by about 0.25 m.
- **The tail panel** sits at `FIN_Y` = 2.8 m, behind the ring, clamped between two cross bars at the spine's upper end. The fin profile is stored relative to `FIN_Y`.
- Side bolts sit at r = 1.645 and patch plates at r ≥ 1.645, both outside the roller flanges' reach (1.606). If you move them inward, they'll clash with the rollers as the ring turns.

## Export to Unity

Run [`desert_monowheel_export.py`](desert_monowheel_export.py) after any change. It's meant to be re-run, and it only reads the `.blend` files.

```
blender --background --python models/vehicles/desert_monowheel_export.py
```

It writes five files to `Assets/Game/Art/Models/Vehicles/Monowheel/`:
- `desert_monowheel_runner.fbx`
- `desert_monowheel_double.fbx`
- `desert_monowheel_double_wide.fbx`
- `desert_monowheel_hauler.fbx`
- `desert_monowheel_patched.fbx`

- **What's kept:** the rig (the ring bones are what spins in game), and the socket empties where players attach.
- **Mirrored parts:** `fix_inverted` repairs, at export time only, the parts that hand edits left mirrored. Three per double variant needed it: `RearRailR`, `SideHoopInnerL` and `SideHoopOuterR`.
- **Verified (2026-09-24):** re-importing each FBX puts every part within 0.1 mm of the source. Each ring is still parented to its `Bone_Ring*`, with its 16 paddles and mounts under it.
