# Gravel Blaster — build record

Handmade spring-driven pipe shotgun for the Gravel Blaster artifact, modelled
from a concept sheet of handmade shotguns (the sheet's main figure: two rusted
pipe barrels, exposed drive springs, forged fork, wooden stock).

## Decomposition

One component file, `gravel_blaster.blend`, holding three variations as
collections — variations of one thing belong in one file:

| Collection | Barrels | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Coll_GravelBlaster_Twin` | 2, level | The hero. **Ships to Unity.** Carries `Marker_Muzzle` / `Marker_Grip`. |
| `Coll_GravelBlaster_Triple` | 3, triangle | Shorter pipes, taller profile — the sheet's "3 barrel mod". Built ahead. |
| `Coll_GravelBlaster_Quad` | 4, 2×2 block | Longest and squarest — the "4 barrel mod". Built ahead. |

The gun is generated by one parameterised `blaster()` function taking the
barrel layout, so the three variations cannot drift apart structurally. No
sub-components were split out into their own files: every part here (pipe
barrel, drive spring, fork) is shaped by this weapon's proportions and has no
plausible reuse elsewhere — the library's generic pipes and springs live at
vehicle scale.

Nothing was reused from the library: `portal_gun` is an extinguisher
silhouette, `drum_magazine` and `pipe_run` are metres across. The
marker-cube convention for carrying the muzzle/grip coordinates across the
FBX is copied from `portal_gun.py`.

## Assembly / geometry facts the Unity side relies on

- Front is **−Y**, up **+Z**; overall length 1.22 m (muzzle −0.700 to butt
  +0.516). Origin at the stock wrist.
- `Marker_Muzzle` at (0, −0.700, 0.030) — centred between the two bores.
- `Marker_Grip` at (0, 0.205, −0.014) — the cloth-wrapped wrist behind the
  trigger.
- Only the Twin is exported (`gravel_blaster_export.py`), to
  `Assets/Game/Art/Models/Weapons/GravelBlaster/gravel_blaster.fbx`.

## Materials

All from the existing palette; nothing added. Index 0 is
`Mat_Metal_Steel_Worn` because `bmesh.ops.bevel` stamps its faces with
index 0. Barrels `Mat_Metal_HullRust_Orange` with `Mat_Metal_Rust_Heavy`
muzzle mouths, mechanism `Mat_Metal_Steel_Dark`, springs and shell rims
`Mat_Metal_Brass_Tarnished`, stock `Mat_Wood_Ply_Worn`, wraps
`Mat_Fabric_Canvas_Faded`, bore shadows `Mat_Neutral_Black_Matte`.

## Judgement calls

- No armature: the trigger and hammer are notionally articulated but the
  artifact presents recoil and blast through particles and transforms on the
  Unity side; nothing on the mesh needs to deform.
- The 1-in-10 backfire and the "compress springs" reload are gameplay
  fiction; the model only has to make them believable (exposed springs,
  visible brass shell rims at the open breech).
- Barrel bore radius, band placement and stock drop were chosen by eye
  against the sheet; the sheet has no dimensions.
