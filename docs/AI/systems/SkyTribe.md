# Sky Tribe

The second tribe on the existing faction stack (no new faction machinery), plus the two things unique to it: a city that drifts through the air with its own NavMesh and a fleet of escorts, and war parties that travel by NPC-flown vessel instead of on foot. This doc is where faction/roster, city and vessels meet; [AgentSystem.md](AgentSystem.md) and [Vehicles.md](Vehicles.md) own the generic mechanisms (factions, `NpcWorldSim`, mounting) this tribe reuses rather than replaces.

## Model

- **Faction.** `SkyTribeFaction.asset` — Neutral `defaultStance`, Neutral to Sand by design (no row in `GlobalRelationships.asset`), registered in `FactionGoodwillLedger.tribes` on `NpcWorldSim`. Authored by `RosterAuthoring.AuthorSkyTribeFaction` (`Tools/SpaceGame/Agents/Author Sky Tribe Faction`), which mirrors onto Sky any row Sand has (none today — the rule is generic, not a special case for "Sand has none").
- **People.** The Sand-nomad body, recoloured: `SkyNomad_{Umber,Tan,Maroon,StrawHat}.prefab` under `Prefabs/Agents/Characters/SkyTribe/`, built by `NomadPrefabBuilder.SkyNomads` (`Build Sky Nomad NPCs`). Cloth is `SkyNomadCloth_*` — pale sky blue (0.58, 0.72, 0.86), cloud-white scarf accent (0.90, 0.91, 0.88) — under its own `MaterialPrefix`, distinct from Sand's `NomadCloth_*`, so the two tribes' builds never recolour each other's meshes even though they share mesh names. Each recipe also sets `WanderReachableOnly`, so a resident's `WanderModule` never targets an unreachable island of the city mesh (Gotchas). Every Sky person (`FliesWithWingPack`, the soldier recipe too) wears a folded wing pack (`EntityBodyEquipment.startingWorn`, drawn by `WornFit`'s folded pose: 2.64 m, the player's worn size, at (0, 0.4, −0.65), −90° about X, flat along the spine) and flies far goals on it (`NpcFlightModule`); with `JoinsGroups` it carries `NpcTaskModule` + `FormationModule`, with `TravelsToGoals` `GoalTravelModule`. The StrawHat alone also wears the Repulsor on its right forearm (`WornGauntlet`, fired by `NpcGauntletUseModule` at RangedAttack).
- **Roster.** `Rosters/SkyTribe.asset`: the five `SkyTribePeople` prefabs (the four nomads and `SkySoldier`) as both Scout and Warrior (10 members), **no Rider role** — a Sky party flies, so it has no mount to leave behind. Hand items are Sand's own seven; `hostileLines` is `SkyTribeHostileLines.asset` (8 lines). War-party tiers (seated riders): 0 = 2 Scouts, 1 = 3 Warriors + 1 Scout, 2 = 5 Warriors + 2 Scouts, **plus 2 / 4 / 6 escort-flier Warriors** (`RoleCount.ownWings`, `RosterAuthoring.SkyEscortFliers`; user decision 2026-10-07) who never take a seat or lead. `SkySoldier` (`Build Sky Soldier NPC`, its own `sky_soldier.fbx` and orange `SkySoldierCloth` palette) wears the wing pack like the nomads, no gauntlet. `AuthorSkyRoster` and `Wire Sky City Settlement` silently skip any `SkyTribePeople` recipe whose `PrefabPath` does not resolve: build a new person before re-running them.
- **The city — a drifting flagship.** `Prefabs/Environment/Structures/SkyFleet/SkyCityFleet.prefab` (art finished — never edit `sky_city.blend`/`.fbx`, or the scale `SkyCityBuilder` sets) is placed in `persistentScene.unity` by `SkyFleetPlacement` and **moves**: an `AgentController` whose brain is [`DriftRouteModule`](Assets/Game/Scripts/Vehicles/SkyVessel/DriftRouteModule.cs) flies it through [`FlyingRigidbodyMotor`](Assets/Game/Scripts/agents/AI/Motors/FlyingRigidbodyMotor.cs) in **kinematic-hull mode** (its 13 non-convex mesh colliders rule out a dynamic body), slowly (2 m/s) round a fixed loop of four waypoints at 280 m, mooring 120 s at each. It carries its own baked NavMesh ([StaticNavMeshData](Assets/Game/Scripts/World/Streaming/NavMesh/StaticNavMeshData.cs) + `SkyCityNavMesh.asset`, baked in prefab space by SkyCityNavMeshBaker), a `WorldSiteMarker` (`Home`, "Sky City", **`airborne = true`**) and a [`SettlementDeck`](Assets/Game/Scripts/Vehicles/SkyVessel/SettlementDeck.cs) that parks its residents while it sails (Flows) — full NavMesh mechanics: [NavMeshSystem.md](NavMeshSystem.md); airborne-site search rules: [TerrainGeneration.md](TerrainGeneration.md). Design, route and defaults: [spec](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-04-drifting-sky-fleet-design.md).
- **The escorts.** Seven scene objects round it (`SkyEscort{i}_{Vessel}`: 2 freighters, 3 skiffs, 2 tugs — instances of `SkyFreighter`/`SkySkiff`/`SkyTug.prefab`), each flown by the same motor under [`FleetEscortModule`](Assets/Game/Scripts/Vehicles/SkyVessel/FleetEscortModule.cs): a station in the flagship's frame plus its own three-axis sine wander (±14 m / ±5 m, 43–73 s periods, per-escort phase), holding the flagship's heading. Not nested in the fleet prefab: a hull under another hull's moving transform cannot steer, and netcode does not support nested scene `NetworkObject`s.
- **Smoke.** Every engine duct (two per `Mesh_SkyCity_SternGear` assembly; the tug has two assemblies) blasts a sooty-grey (0.30, 0.29, 0.27, alpha 0.45) `DustCloudRecipe` cloud astern as a jet — `SkyFleetMovers.AddSmoke` overrides the recipe's thrown-sand speed, cone and drag with 12–18 m/s × √(duct scale), a 10° cone and a constant 4.5 m/s² × √(duct scale) braking (the trail runs ~25 m astern, ~50 m on the city), with white particle colour because JetSmoke multiplies its tint by it — the recipe's defaults stay the monowheel's; [`EngineSmoke`](Assets/Game/Scripts/Vehicles/SkyVessel/EngineSmoke.cs) drives the rate from the hull's measured speed (1 → 4 puffs/s per duct), faded out between 600 and 1500 m from the camera.
- **Loot (design).** One variant in four wears the Repulsor and a sky-wing is two scouts: few enough faucets that the gauntlet stays a find, not a drop on every corpse (GDC-L1-ECON-0001 faucets vs drains, GDC-L1-ECON-0002 scarcity); both are worn visibly, so the player can read who carries what before the fight (GDC-L1-SYS-0006).
- **A wing pack drops only from a Sky person killed while flying, or from a war-party flier** (user decision 2026-10-07, superseding "every Sky corpse drops its pack"); anyone killed on foot keeps it on (`EntityLootTable.DropsWorn`). D7 still makes the pack the only route to flight — the patrol, the swarm and every war party's escort keep it obtainable — and a war flier drops its gun only on `NpcGroupTransport.flierWeaponDropChance` (0.25), so a big escort is not a gun pile (ECON-0001/0002).
- **The patrol** (`sky-patrol`, user 2026-10-07: "sometimes see flyers flying idle through the sky in formation"): the airborne twin of the Sand riders' caravans — one seeded group, a leader + 2–4 wingmen (seeded count) in a chevron, always airborne, flying a ~5 km figure of eight waypoints through the low basin (circling each 0–40 s): past the player's spawn point about 130 m off twice a lap and within ~250 m of the Sand camps once, a lap about every 7 min. Never lands (v1, user decision). Mechanics: [AgentSystem.md](AgentSystem.md) (`NpcAirPatrol`). Missable ambience by design (GDC-L1-LEVEL-0007; drift, lag and banking for life, GDC-L1-ANIM-0005).
- **The swarm** (user 2026-10-07: "the flyers in the sky cities can also swarm around the sky cities"): while the city is moored and a player is within 900 m, up to 3 residents go up and circle it, sometimes in pairs, then land back on the promenade — `SettlementLoiterFlights` (Flows).
- **Vessels.** `SkySkiffTransport` (4 seats) and `SkyFreighterTransport` (8 seats), NPC-only — no player seat, camera or controls, but players can shoot at them. Built from the escort FBX models (the drifting escorts are separate prefabs that only keep station) by SkyVesselBuilder (`Tools/SpaceGame/Vehicles/Build Sky Transports`); server-flown by [VesselPilot](Assets/Game/Scripts/Vehicles/SkyVessel/VesselPilot.cs) — full component API: [Vehicles.md](Vehicles.md).

## Flows

**Author + build order** (idempotent; re-run after any recipe change): `Author Sky Tribe Faction` → `Build Sky Nomad NPCs` (pass 1, no roster yet) → `Author Sky Tribe Roster` (needs the prefabs' baked faction) → `Build Sky Nomad NPCs` (pass 2, bakes `roster.handItems`) → `Build Sky Fleet Prefabs` (rebakes the city NavMesh, re-runs `Wire Sky City Settlement`) → `Place Sky Fleet In World` → `Build Sky Transports` → `Wire War Party Templates` (`WireWorldSim` adds/updates `sky-war-party`: transport = skiff/freighter, `travelSpeed` = the slower of the two prefabs' `VesselPilot.cruiseSpeed`, `homeSiteName` = `WorldSite.SkyCityName`) → `Wire Sky Wing` → `Wire Sky Patrol`.

**Drift** — server only. `DriftRouteModule` waits out its mooring, then `MoveTo`s the next waypoint; `FlyingRigidbodyMotor` ramps up at 0.05 m/s² to 2 m/s, turns the bow toward its travel at a slerp rate of 0.05/s (it crabs a little while it turns — a drift, not a drive), brakes at 0.12 m/s² inside `arriveRadius`, and the module moors once it is slower than 0.2 m/s. Each escort's `FleetEscortModule` re-aims every frame, so the escorts follow the turn on their own, each slightly differently.

**The deck under way** — `SettlementDeck` (server): on departure every Sky resident (`SettlementPopulation.CollectPeople`) standing inside `DeckVolume` and not already carried by anything is parked where it stands — `NpcSeating.Suppress` (NavMeshAgent and motor off, `RidesAsPassenger`, body held kinematic: it still sees and shoots) and `NpcSeating.Attach` under the flagship's `NetworkObject` — then `StaticNavMeshData` is disabled (no NavMesh left in mid-air) and the population's clock holds. At the mooring it re-enables `StaticNavMeshData` (which adds the mesh at the hull's new pose), unparks everyone (`Detach`, `Restore` warps the agent onto the mesh) and lets the clock run. Players on any deck are carried by the hull's `WalkerPlatformCarrier`.

**City population** — server/offline only. Every `spawnInterval` (45 s, faster during the 4 `initialWaves`), `SettlementPopulation` counts Sky residents inside `countRadius` and, below the cap, samples the ring and keeps only points `NavMeshReach.CanWalk` reaches from `PromenadeAnchor` (see [NavMeshSystem.md](NavMeshSystem.md) for how much of the mesh that actually is). Because the fleet lives in `persistentScene` rather than a chunk scene, `keepGroundChunksLoaded` registers the fleet root as a tracked transform, holds it `SuspendAnchor`ed until the streamer's own initial chunks load (so it never competes with the session's own spawn-area preload), then resumes it and waits for the chunks under `countRadius` before starting the spawn clock — skip this and a reload spawns a second population beside the one the save restores (measured: 16 → 24).

**Flight** — server only; the mechanics are [NpcFlight.md](NpcFlight.md). Never takes off in a fight (D2). **Sortie:** a resident standing on the **moored** city with no goal every `sortieCheckInterval` (20 s; was 30) rolls `sortieChance` (0.2; was 0.08; a won roll whose launch the deck refuses stays pending 10 s) and flies down to a Ruin within 1500 m (`sortieTask`; with no Ruin in the world yet, a ground NavMesh point 250–600 m off — `sortieRoamBand`, never the city's own deck — so a player at the moored city watches it fly down and land), alone and unsaved. **Sky-wing:** no Ruin, ScrapField or Camp site exists in the world yet (only the city's Home), so every stop resolves to a roam point 230–700 m off (`NpcTaskPlanner.TryRoamPoint`: a third of `searchRadius` 700 to all of it, no log) and the pair wanders the desert, dwelling 20–45 s at each; a matching site, once one exists, wins. The group walks a stop's last metres and flies every leg past `minFlightDistance` — each scout alone to the shared goal (D5) — then lands and walks (`GoalTravelModule`). Freshly spawned, each scout stands `launchDelayAfterSpawn` (4 s) before its first take-off, and the group is not folded while a scout is in the air unless every player is beyond `NpcWorldSim.airborneFoldRadius` (750 m) — it used to fold ~100 m into its first leg and its craft vanished in mid-air ([NpcFlight.md](NpcFlight.md) Gotchas). Folded, its record moves at 22 m/s like any group.

**Patrol** — server only: see [AgentSystem.md](AgentSystem.md) (`NpcAirPatrol`). Spawned within `airPatrol.spawnRadius` (550 m) already flying at cruise height, folded only past `airborneFoldRadius` (750 m), since a pilot is always aloft.

**Swarm** — server only (`SettlementLoiterFlights`): a resident on the moored deck with no target, goal, sortie or parking takes off on the component's order (`NpcFlightModule.TakeOffNow`) and flies an orbit station on the city's own transform (`NpcAviator.Escort`); a wingman flies a station on its lead and takes an orbit of its own when the lead heads in. Time up and moored: it cruises to an approach fix outside a free pad whose straight-in is clear (`Physics.SphereCast`), then `LandOnDeck`s ([NpcFlight.md](NpcFlight.md) Gotchas: the deck is no ground to the probe). Never lands under way — the deck's NavMesh is withdrawn — so the city setting off under it carries the orbit along (the swarm under way) and it lands at the next mooring. No pad for `maxLoiterSeconds`: put down on the ground below, logged. Fliers beyond `countRadius` are `SettlementPopulation.AwayResidents`, so the city does not replace them while they circle.

**War-party transport** (`sky-war-party`, driven by `NpcWorldSim`/`WarPartyDirector`):
1. **Raise.** `WarPartyDirector.ChooseOrigin` returns `WorldSiteRegistry.TryFindByName("Sky City")` (any kind, airborne, observed or not) instead of the usual nearest-camp search.
2. **Travel, folded.** The record moves at `NpcGroupTransport.travelSpeed` (28 m/s) and catches up like any party — never abandoned for distance while still flying (Gotchas).
3. **Board.** Inside `spawnRadius`, `NpcWorldSim.Spawn` counts riders with `NpcGroupComposition.Rides` and picks `VesselFor(count)` (tier 0/1 → skiff, tier 2 → freighter). `BoardTransport` first tries `TryTakeParkedHull` — an empty, undamaged, `Done` hull of that prefab within `dockReuseRadius` (150 m) — else spawns fresh at `FirstFreeDock`, `RiseToCruise`d before the network spawn so nobody watches it climb out of the ground, nose toward `QuarryPoint`. Riders spawn under the hull and seat exactly as on foot, except that each is made `seated` (`NpcSpawn.Create(..., seated: true)`): its `NavMeshAgent` is already off when it wakes at cruise height (Gotchas). **Escort fliers** (`PlannedMember.OwnWings`, never counted by `Rides`) are made seated in mid-air on a ring round the hull and, once it is launched, take off at once (`WarPartyEscorts.TakeOff` → `NpcFlightModule.TakeOffInAir`: the spawn is an order, so no launch delay, flight distance or D2 check) and keep `NpcGroupTransport.EscortOffset` stations on it (`escortRadius` 40, `escortHeight` 12; `NpcAviator.Escort`), shooting from the cradle if provoked. When the vessel goes `Descend`/`Unload`, is wrecked or gone, or has delivered, `WarPartyEscorts.Steer` lands each `escortLandingSpread` (30 m) round the drop; from then they fight on foot and later flights keep D2. A run turning for home still loaded keeps its escort. A war flier is `EntityLootTable.MarkWarFlier`ed. `GiveUpOnStrays` never runs while the party is in flight, so an escort is no stray.
4. **Launch.** `pilot.Begin(dock, () => QuarryPoint(group), riders, despawnRadius)` — dock is `NpcGroupTransport.DockPoint`, `despawnRadius` is `NpcWorldSim.despawnRadius` (350 m), never a prefab literal. The pilot then runs `Cruise → Approach → Descend → Unload → Climb → Depart → Done` (landing/hover math: [Vehicles.md](Vehicles.md)). It chooses no landing site until `WorldStreamer.IsGroundLoadedAround(quarry, landing.ringMax + footprintRadius)` — until then it keeps flying at the quarry (Gotchas).
5. **Delivered?** Polled every tick, not event-driven (`Finished` never fires for a mid-run despawn): `NpcGroupTransport.IsDelivered(vesselExists, wrecked, aboard)` — true once nobody is seated, the hull is wrecked, or it is gone. **`Done` alone is not delivery** — a run with no landing site anywhere flies home still carrying the party. While undelivered and `Done`, `TransportParkedFor` accumulates; once it reaches `transportRetryDelay` (15 s), `ShouldRelaunch` re-`Begin`s the same hull from the dock. Once delivered, the group is an ordinary spawned party and the empty hull despawns once every player is beyond `despawnRadius`.
6. **Fold while aboard.** The vessel despawns **first** (`OnNetworkDespawn` unseats riders onto the NavMesh below), then the members — never the other order, or Netcode lifts seated children to the scene root with nothing to release them ([Vehicles.md](Vehicles.md) Gotchas). `Delivered` stays false; the group flies on from the vessel's last position.
7. **Release/restore.** A released or wiped-out group is not removed while `Transport` is still out (`ReleaseGroup`/`DisbandWhenFolded` wait for it). Restore: undelivered spawns in a vessel again; delivered walks. With no usable vessel prefab, the sim logs an error and spawns the party on foot (`Delivered = true`).

## Multiplayer

The flagship and every escort are **in-scene** `NetworkObject`s with the sky transports' stack (`NetRelay`, `NetAuthority`, server-authority `NetworkTransform`, kinematic body): the server's `AgentController` + motor fly them, `NetAuthority` switches both off on clients, which interpolate. Parked residents are parented under the flagship's `NetworkObject`, which replicates. `EngineSmoke` and `WalkerPlatformCarrier` run on every machine from the transform each sees, so a client's own player is carried by its own copy of the deck. `SettlementDeck`'s parking runs only where `Network.Decides`; a client's copy of the city NavMesh stays where the scene put it, which nothing on a client walks. Its presentation half (`RefreshPresented`, on `OnTransformChildrenChanged`) runs on every machine: a client is handed only the parenting, never the `Suppress`, so it parks the `AgentGroundConform` of whoever netcode parents under the hull and gives it back when they are taken off — as `VesselSeats` does for crew. All four fleet prefabs are registered network prefabs (every root-`NetworkObject` prefab is).

Every war-party decision is server-side: `NpcWorldSim`/`WarPartyDirector` (`Network.Decides`), `VesselPilot.Update` (`Network.Simulates`). Vessels are `NetworkObject`s with a server-authority `NetworkTransform`; clients interpolate. Riders seat via `NpcSeating.Attach`/`TrySetParent` under the hull's `NetworkObject`, replicated the same way `NpcPassenger` seats a caravan mount — a seated rider keeps its own `AgentTargeting`/`PerceptionModule` and fires from the saddle. Both transport prefabs are registered network prefabs (`NetworkPrefabRegistrar.Sync`).

## Persistence

The flagship and escorts are authored `persistentScene` objects with **baked** identities (`sky-city-flagship`, `sky-escort-0..6` — baked so that nobody reordering `persistentScene`'s roots orphans their records), so their pose is captured by `TransformSaveable` under `persistent` and re-applied through `SaveTeleport`; `MotorStateSaveable`, `AgentPacingSaveable` and `AgentGoalSaveable` come with the `AgentController`. Where the flagship is on its loop is `DriftRouteSaveable` (key `driftRoute`: leg, under way, mooring time left). The escorts' wander is a pure function of time and their serialized phase: nothing more to save. A resident parked at save time is saved like any resident (filed under `persistent`, since it is parented into that scene) and re-parked by `SettlementDeck`'s scan after the load, or simply walks if the city was moored.

Faction, roster and prefabs are authored assets, not save state. City residents are ordinary `SettlementPopulation` spawns (`GameServices.World.Spawn`), saved once migrated into the chunk scene under the city — hence `keepGroundChunksLoaded` above; see [Persistence.md](Persistence.md) and the "settlement outside the chunk scenes" gotcha in [AgentSystem.md](AgentSystem.md). **Vessels and their seated riders are never saved individually** — only the war-party group record is, and `NpcGroup.Record.delivered` (appended 2026-09-17; an older save reads `false`) decides what comes back: an undelivered party spawns in a fresh vessel and tries again, a delivered one is on foot. **Fliers are not saved** (D4, [NpcFlight.md](NpcFlight.md)): a sortie flier is gone after a load; a `sky-wing` caught in the air comes back on foot, because its record is ground-projected (`NpcWorldSim.GroundedPosition`); the `sky-patrol` comes back in the air (its spawn always lifts it), from a record whose `goalPosition` is its current waypoint — no new field; a war party's escort comes back with its undelivered party in the air, or on foot with a delivered one; a swarm flier is withheld for its flight and given back when it lands alive (saved mid-flight it is gone and the population refills it). `SettlementLoiterFlights` holds no saved state. The worn gear is `npcWorn` (`EntityBodyEquipmentSaveable`).

## Gotchas

- **The city was never in the world on this branch.** Main's cleanup (8e7ef62a) moved the fleet prefabs into `SkyFleet/` and deleted the `persistentScene` instance; the merge kept the deletion, and every Sky builder still pointed at the old paths, so a rebuild would have written new prefabs (new GUIDs) beside the moved ones. Worse, the deleted instance stood at (3704, 228, 1182), where the world NavMesh has ground at y 251–308: a rock spire through the city's decks. `SkyFleetPrefabTests.BuilderPath_NamesTheShippedAsset` pins the paths to the GUIDs; `EveryLeg_ClearsTheGroundUnderTheWholeFleet` holds every leg's ±160 m corridor 25 m clear of the fleet's 45 m draught (a 229 m rock 170 m north of the first draft of leg 0 failed it).
- **A moving hull must carry no static flags.** A batching-static mesh is combined where it stood at scene load and stays there while its transform flies off; occluder data is baked for a pose too. `SkyCityBuilder` and `SkyFleetBuilder` no longer call `StaticPropBuilder.MarkStatic`; `Hull_HasNoStaticFlags_SoItsMeshesMoveWithIt` guards all four prefabs.
- **A NavMeshAgent cannot ride a moving NavMesh** — there is no API to move a `NavMeshDataInstance`, and re-adding one every frame resets every path. Hence parking under way and re-laying at the moorings (Flows). Anything that samples the city's NavMesh while it sails finds nothing (the mesh is withdrawn); `SettlementPopulation.SpawningSuspended` keeps the city's own spawner off it.
- **`SkyCitySettlementWiring` used to fail outright on an unbuilt recipe** (the sky soldier, before it was built), and `Build Sky Fleet Prefabs` saves the fleet from scratch first — so a rebuild left the fleet with no site, alarm or population and only a line in the report. It now skips unbuilt recipes, as `AuthorSkyRoster` does.
- **Docks and return flights do not follow the city.** `NpcGroupTransport.DockPoint` and a vessel's `Home` are computed from the site position at launch; a war party returning after the city moored elsewhere flies back to where it left from (at most one leg, < 1.2 km). The site marker itself follows the hull (`SettlementDeck` refreshes it).
- **Engine smoke is a jet, not thrown dust.** With the recipe's 4–7.5 m/s puffs and drag 2.5, smoke from a hull doing 2–6 m/s stopped a metre or two out and piled up as a ball at the nozzle (seen in playtest 2026-10-04); coal-black also read as ink, and a 0.30 grey tint still rendered near-black because `JetSmoke` multiplies `_Color` by the particle colour (tint × tint). Particle drag with `multiplyDragByParticleVelocity` on (the recipe's) is quadratic and stops a 15 m/s jet within ~8 m; off, it is a constant deceleration. Override in `AddSmoke`, never in `DustCloudRecipe`, which the monowheel's dust shares.
- **The city keeps its ground chunks loaded wherever it drifts** (`keepGroundChunksLoaded` tracks the root), so it streams chunks in and out along its route, one chunk border every few minutes.

- **A `Home` site is not a ground destination by default.** The city sails 280 m up with no ground link; its marker's `airborne` flag keeps every kind-based ground search (`NpcTaskModule`/`NpcTaskPlanner`/`WarPartyDirector`'s camp search) from sending an unrelated ground NPC there. Only `TryFindByName("Sky City")` (what the transport flow uses) or `includeAirborne: true` finds it — [TerrainGeneration.md](TerrainGeneration.md).
- **Most of the city's NavMesh is unreachable islands** (roofs, gas-bag tops, ladder-only decks — ~120 welded islands, only ~3 300 m² of promenade actually connected). Both the population's spawn ring and each resident's `WanderModule` filter through `NavMeshReach.CanWalk`, or NPCs stand on a roof they can never leave, or a wander pick lands a partial path from a railing (`onlyReachableDestinations` + a back-off timer on a failed pick — [NavMeshSystem.md](NavMeshSystem.md)).
- **A war party never gives up on distance while it is still flying.** Ordinary parties abandon beyond `maxPursuitDistance`, but a Sky party's home site can be 1.5+ km from a moving quarry — `WarPartyRules.ShouldAbandon(..., inFlight: sim.IsInFlight(party))` exempts it. Tried the other way (`Track` before the abandon check) and still lost a party raised 1.6 km out on the very next step; the exemption is deliberate, not a shortcut.
- **A vessel waits for the ground before it looks for a landing site.** A party restored by a load is spawned in the world's first frame, before a single chunk scene has streamed in, and often already inside `approachDistance` — so its pilot went `Approach` and ran `LandingSiteFinder.Find` over empty space the very next frame, found no site, aborted and flew home; the group folded out of view and came back ~20 s later (measured 2026-09-17: hull `NEW → Cruise → Approach → Depart` inside frames 7971–7973, all 9 chunk samples round the quarry unloaded). `VesselPilot.MaintainSite` now skips choosing and re-checking while `IsGroundLoadedAround` is false; `LandingSiteFinder` stays pure and never asks about streaming. No `WorldStreamer` (a test scene) means nothing to wait for.
- **A rider is spawned with its `NavMeshAgent` already off, not switched off in `beforeSpawn`.** Unity logs "Failed to create agent because it is not close enough to the NavMesh" from *inside* `Instantiate` when an enabled agent wakes off the mesh — measured: the warning lands between the lines before and after `Instantiate`, and `NavMeshAgentMotor.Awake` has already set `enabled = false` by the time it returns. So `beforeSpawn` is too late; `NpcSpawn.Create(..., seated: true)` instantiates under an inactive cradle, disables the agents, then releases the NPC to the scene root. `NpcSeating.Suppress` does not record an agent it finds off, so unseating does not hand it back directly — the re-enabled `NavMeshAgentMotor`'s reattach (`ReattachInterval` 0.5 s) puts it on the NavMesh, exactly as it did when `Awake` switched it off.
- **A shot-down (wrecked) vessel drops its passengers straight down, never at a chosen site** — the fallback used to teleport them to a landing site the vessel had already picked, sometimes 100+ m away and into another vessel's footprint.
- **A hull must despawn its seated riders before it despawns itself**, or Netcode lifts them to the scene root with `RidesAsPassenger` still on and nothing ever releases them. `VesselPilot.OnNetworkDespawn` (server) unseats everyone first; `NpcWorldSim.DespawnTransport` always runs before `DespawnMembers`.
- **Two parked hulls never overlap.** `NpcGroupTransport.DockPoint` rings dock slots `dockSpacing` (45 m, ≥2× the freighter's footprint) apart, and a new arrival reuses a parked, undamaged, empty hull of the right prefab before spawning another — otherwise every returning party leaves a hull idling at the same dock point.
- **A transport's bow is not the model's fixed −Z.** The escort FBXs disagree with each other on which end is which (mesh names like `SternGear`/`Prow` are inherited from an unrelated part library and do not track it); `SkyVesselBuilder.Transport.ModelYawCorrection` is measured per vessel from the raw FBX's own geometry (both 0 today), not assumed. Seats/`Ramp`/`Drop` need no separate fix — they are read in or computed from the model's own (rotated) space.
- **A group seeded at a site kind nobody registered starts under the terrain.** `startNearSite` with no such site falls back to the sim's own origin (y 0); the `sky-wing` was seeded at a Ruin that does not exist. It uses `useStartPosition` with a NavMesh-verified point instead.
- **Never rewrite `SkyCityBuilder`/`SkyFleetBuilder` wholesale** — the sky city art is finished; extend them (as the NavMesh bake and settlement wiring do, chained onto the end of `Build`) and never edit `sky_city.blend`/`.fbx`.
- **The transports stay unwired because they carry a `VesselPilot`.** Their `HealthComponent` and `EntityFaction` each qualify for `SaveablePolicy.NeedsSaving`, and `HealthComponent` alone made `RagdollWiring` call them bodies, so every builder chaining `Wire Saveable Prefabs` → `RagdollWiring` re-added `SaveableEntity`, four savers, `RagdollRig` and `AgentRagdoll` to both prefabs until 2026-09-24. `NeedsSaving` and `RagdollWiring.IsBody` now refuse any root with a `VesselPilot`; a new vessel is covered by carrying one. `SkyTransportPrefabTests.TheVesselCarriesNoSaversAndNoRagdoll` reads both prefabs off disk.
- **The fleet's hulls stay unragdolled because they carry a `WalkerPlatformCarrier`.** `SkyCityFleet`, `SkySkiff`, `SkyFreighter` and `SkyTug` are flown through an `AgentController`, which alone makes `RagdollWiring.HasDrivenSkeleton` true, have no `VesselPilot`, and live under `Environment/Structures/SkyFleet/`, outside both vehicle folders — so `Wire Prefabs` gave every root a `RagdollRig` + `AgentRagdoll` (removed 2026-10-06). A blast could make the whole city go limp, and the player's ledge probe read each deck as a character and refused to climb it. `RagdollWiring.IsBody` now refuses any root with a `WalkerPlatformCarrier` — a deck people ride, which no creature has (today: the fleet, DesertCrawler, DuneFoil, RigWalker, PlayerShip, the DuneBarges). `SkyFleetPrefabTests.Hull_IsNotARagdollBody` and `FleetPrefab_CarriesNoRagdoll` guard it.
- **Chain a builder to its end, never to `SyncMenu()`.** Any menu that must run to completion in one pass (`Build Sky Nomad NPCs`, `Build Sky Transports`) calls `NetworkPrefabRegistrar.Sync(out _, out _)`, not `SyncMenu()` — the latter opens a modal "OK" dialog that parks the rest of the chain (savers, ragdolls) until a human clicks it.

- **A group's centroid is mid-air while it flies: ground it or a fold respawns it in the sky.** `CurrentPosition` used to return the raw centroid; a sky-wing folded mid-flight then spawned 60 m up and fell. `GroundedPosition` keeps a NavMesh-near point and otherwise casts down (`PhysicsGroundProbe.TryGroundBelow`).
- **The city's NavMesh is withdrawn under way, so residents launch only while moored.** A sortie needs a resident standing inside the airborne Home site; under way they are parked off the deck (The deck under way).

## Extending

- **A new roster role or person:** add a `NomadRecipe` to `NomadPrefabBuilder` (its own `ClothPalette.MaterialPrefix` — reusing one recolours the wrong tribe's cloth), build its prefab, append it where `SkyTribePeople` is read, then re-run `Author Sky Tribe Roster`.
- **A new tribe with its own city and vessels:** follow the [spacegame-tribe](.claude/skills/spacegame-tribe/SKILL.md) skill end to end (§9 covers a home settlement) and the [spacegame-vessel](.claude/skills/spacegame-vessel/SKILL.md) skill for a new NPC transport; give the template an `NpcGroupTransport` pointing at the new vessels and a `homeSiteName` naming the new site.
- **Another structure outside the chunk scenes that needs walking NPCs:** the NavMesh bake pattern in [NavMeshSystem.md](NavMeshSystem.md) Extending, plus the three `SettlementPopulation` options (`reachableFrom`, `keepGroundChunksLoaded`, `initialWaves`) covered here and in [AgentSystem.md](AgentSystem.md).
- **Another flying tribe:** set `FliesWithWingPack` (and `JoinsGroups`) on its recipe; `ConfigureFlightAndGear` does the rest.
- **Tuning flight/landing/capacity:** every number here is serialized on `VesselPilot`, `NpcGroupTransport` or `NpcWorldSim` — see [Vehicles.md](Vehicles.md) for the full tunable list.
